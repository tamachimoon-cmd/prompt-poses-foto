# 242 — Três pontos com key quente e fill frio

Finalidade: headshot de estúdio com separação por luz de recorte e contraste de temperatura de cor.

## Prompt (copiar)

Chest-up studio headshot of an adult professional in her late 30s, shoulders angled 30 degrees to camera-left, face turned to the lens, calm confident expression with a slight closed-mouth smile, charcoal wool blazer over a white crew-neck top, three-point lighting: soft warm key light (about 3800K) from camera-left at 45 degrees and slightly above eye level, subtle cool-toned fill (about 6000K) from camera-right at roughly one third of the key's intensity, narrow rim light from behind camera-right outlining the hair and shoulder for edge separation, clean light-grey background, 85mm lens at f/2.0, sharp focus on the eyes with natural catchlights, natural skin texture with visible pores and fine lines, true-to-life skin tone, professional corporate photography, 4:5 aspect ratio, no plastic skin, no airbrushing, no beauty filter, no blown highlights.

## Por que funciona

Nomear as três luzes pela função (key, fill, rim) e dar direção e intensidade relativa evita a luz "chapada" de estúdio genérico. A diferença de temperatura entre key quente e fill frio dá volume sem precisar de contraste duro. Pedir poros e linhas finas combate a pele de plástico.

# 516 — Natural Stance Outdoor Weight Shift

Finalidade: pose natural e relaxada para retratos outdoor lifestyle.

## Prompt (copiar)

A woman standing straight with feet slightly apart, weight on one leg, right hip slightly cocked, hands relaxed by her sides, soft natural smile, in a natural outdoor setting with trees and soft daylight, realistic photography style, full-body shot, eye-level, 50mm lens, photorealistic, natural skin texture --ar 3:4

## Por que funciona

Deslocamento de peso e quadril levemente elevado evitam postura rígida. Mãos soltas e sorriso suave transmitem naturalidade real.

# 315 — Hands in Pockets Casual Lean

Finalidade: pose descontraída e cool, boa para street style.

## Prompt (copiar)

Full-body photograph of an adult model leaning casually against a wall, one leg bent with foot against the wall, both hands in front pockets, thumbs visible, shoulders relaxed, slight smile or neutral, rustic or urban alley, soft natural light, 35mm lens f/2, realistic street photography, authentic relaxed vibe.

## Por que funciona

O lean + mãos no bolso comunicam descontração imediata; um ponto de contato com a parede ancora a pose.

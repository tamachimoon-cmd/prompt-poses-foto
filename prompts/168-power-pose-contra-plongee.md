# 168 — Power pose em contra-plongée

Finalidade: três quartos, câmera baixa, autoridade.

## Prompt (copiar)

Low-angle three-quarter photograph of an adult model, feet shoulder-width, weight even, hands in trouser pockets with thumbs out, shoulders pulled back, chin lifted about 10 degrees, gaze straight into the lens, mouth closed and neutral, navy blazer over a white shirt, dark grey studio backdrop, single key light from camera-left, crisp catchlights, 35mm lens at f/4, sharp focus on the eyes, natural skin texture, editorial photography, no distortion of the hands inside the pockets.

## Por que funciona

Polegares para fora definem a mão mesmo no bolso. Contra-plongée alonga sem adjetivo "poderoso".

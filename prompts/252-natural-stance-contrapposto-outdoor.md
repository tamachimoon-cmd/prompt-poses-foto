# 252 — Natural stance contrapposto outdoor

Finalidade: pose natural de corpo inteiro ou three-quarter com contrapposto clássico.

## Prompt (copiar)

Photorealistic outdoor portrait of an adult model standing with weight shifted onto one leg, opposite hip slightly raised, shoulders relaxed and counter-rotated, hands loose at sides, soft natural daylight, 50mm lens, eye level, visible skin texture, slight film grain, unposed documentary style, feet slightly apart.

## Por que funciona

Contrapposto é a base de poses naturais há séculos; descrever o peso e o contrarrotação evita a pose "boneco" típica de IA.

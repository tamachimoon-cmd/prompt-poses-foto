# 471 — Walking Mid-Stride Street

Finalidade: pose natural de caminhada em rua, capturando movimento realista.

## Prompt (copiar)

Full-body street-style photo of an adult walking across a quiet pedestrian plaza. Capture the moment when the front foot has just touched the ground and the back heel is lifted. Arms swing gently opposite to the legs. Head upright, gaze ahead. Slight side angle, whole body and both shoes visible, space in front of the subject. Soft daylight, simple background, 35mm lens, realistic photography --ar 2:3

## Por que funciona

Descreve o momento exato do passo e o balanço oposto dos braços. Evita poses estáticas e gera naturalidade de movimento.

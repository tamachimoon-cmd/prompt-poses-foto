# Action Leap

**Prompt:**

A person leaping across a gap in mid-air, front leg reaching forward almost straight, back leg trailing with the knee bent and heel raised, right arm driving forward and up, left arm swung back, torso leaning into the jump, eyes on the landing ahead, plain background.

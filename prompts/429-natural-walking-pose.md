# 429 — Natural Walking Pose

Finalidade: captura de caminhada natural em rua, com movimento de braços oposto às pernas.

## Prompt (copiar)

Create a full-body street-style photo of an adult model walking across a quiet pedestrian plaza. Capture the moment when the front foot has just touched the ground and the back heel is lifted. Let the arms swing gently in opposite directions to the legs. Keep the head upright and gaze ahead. Photograph from a slight side angle with the whole body and both shoes visible. Leave space in front of the subject. Soft daylight and a simple background, photorealistic.

## Por que funciona

Momento específico (pé da frente no chão, calcanhar de trás levantado) + balanço de braços gera movimento crível sem blur excessivo.

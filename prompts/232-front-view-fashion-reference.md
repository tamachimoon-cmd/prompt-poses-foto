# 232 — Front view fashion reference

Finalidade: vista frontal limpa para catálogo ou referência de figurino.

## Prompt (copiar)

Create a full-body fashion reference photo of an adult model wearing a contemporary outfit with clear garment details. The model stands facing the camera, with feet hip-width apart and weight distributed evenly. Hold the arms slightly away from the torso so the waistline and sleeves remain visible. Keep both hands relaxed with fingers pointing downward. Include the entire head and shoes, leaving a small margin around the figure. Plain light gray studio background, even lighting, no accessories covering the garment, photorealistic.

## Por que funciona

Braços afastados evitam esconder o corte da roupa. Distribuição igual de peso e margens garantem o corpo inteiro visível para lookbook.

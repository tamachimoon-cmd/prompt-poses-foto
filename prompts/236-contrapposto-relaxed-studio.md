# 236 — Contrapposto relaxed studio

Finalidade: contrapposto relaxado em estúdio para editorial de moda ou lookbook natural.

## Prompt (copiar)

Full-length studio photograph of an adult model in relaxed contrapposto, weight on back leg, opposite knee softly bent, left hip elevated, right shoulder dropped, one hand on hip with relaxed fingers, other arm hanging with natural gap, chin level, direct gaze, softbox key from left, light gray seamless, 70mm f/5.6, photorealistic fabric and skin texture, both feet visible

## Por que funciona

Contrapposto clássico cria S-curve natural. Micro-ajustes de ombro e quadril evitam pose de manequim. Softbox controlada destaca o figurino.

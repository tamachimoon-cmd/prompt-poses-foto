# Template de cena fotorrealista (plano, luz, ângulo, lente)

## Uso

Template base para qualquer foto realista: tipo de plano + sujeito + cenário + luz + ângulo de câmera + lente.

## Prompt

```text
A photorealistic [type of shot] of a [subject description] in a [setting description]. [Description of the light]. Shot from a [camera angle] with a [lens type].
```

## Fonte

- Google AI for Developers — Gemini API: Nano Banana image generation (Prompting guide and strategies)
- https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/image-generation

## Dicas

Exemplo preenchido da própria fonte: "A photorealistic wide-angle shot of a vibrant coral reef teeming with tropical fish. Crystal-clear turquoise water with sunbeams filtering down from the surface, illuminating a sea turtle gliding gracefully over the coral. Shot from a low perspective with a wide-angle lens. Aspect ratio 16:9."

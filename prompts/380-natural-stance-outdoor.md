# 380 — Natural Stance Outdoor

Finalidade: pose natural em pé para retratos outdoor lifestyle, com peso deslocado para evitar rigidez.

## Prompt (copiar)

Photorealistic full-body photograph of an adult woman standing straight with feet slightly apart, weight shifted onto one leg, opposite knee softly bent, hands relaxed by her sides with natural finger spacing, soft natural smile, in a sunlit outdoor park setting with soft foliage bokeh, realistic photography style, natural daylight from the side, 50mm lens at f/2.8, eye-level camera, true-to-life skin texture and clothing fabric, both feet fully visible, no distorted limbs.

## Por que funciona

O deslocamento de peso cria contrapposto sutil e natural. Mãos soltas evitam tensão. Ambiente outdoor com bokeh separa o sujeito sem competir.

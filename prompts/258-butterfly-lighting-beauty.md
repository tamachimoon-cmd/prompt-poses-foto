# 258 — Beauty butterfly lighting

Finalidade: glam suave para beauty ou skincare.

Fórmula: butterfly overhead + reflector de preenchimento + close-up.

## Prompt (copiar)

Beauty close-up portrait of an adult model with clean skin, butterfly lighting from a large softbox directly overhead and slightly in front creating a soft shadow under the nose, reflector fill from below opening the shadows under the chin and eyes, looking straight at camera with neutral expression, soft focus background, 85mm lens at f/2.8, sharp eyes, smooth yet natural skin texture, professional beauty photography, no harsh shadows.

## Por que funciona

Butterfly é o setup de beauty por excelência. Descrever a posição overhead + fill evita o “flat lighting”.

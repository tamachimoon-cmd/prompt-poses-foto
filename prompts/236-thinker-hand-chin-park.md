# 236 — Thinker hand on chin park

Finalidade: pose contemplativa em ambiente outdoor com outono.

## Prompt (copiar)

Photograph of an adult man standing with one hand resting on his chin, looking thoughtful, in a park with autumn leaves, soft overcast light, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, natural skin texture, relaxed posture, photorealistic documentary style.

## Por que funciona

Mão no queixo dá função clara; 85mm + overcast + folhas de outono criam bokeh e mood sem drama excessivo.

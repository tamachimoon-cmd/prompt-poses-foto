# 206 — Beauty Close-up Visible Pores

Finalidade: close de beleza com textura real, sem retouch plástico.

## Prompt (copiar)

Close portrait of a fictional woman in her thirties with short black hair and light brown skin, looking at the camera. Visible pores and a small scar on her left eyebrow. Soft window light from the left, 85mm lens, f/1.8, shallow depth of field, neutral grey wall. Natural skin, no makeup shine, no text.

## Por que funciona

Imperfeição específica (scar + pores) + luz de janela + sem "flawless" ou "perfect". Modelos modernos (Flux, GPT Image) respondem melhor a detalhes concretos.

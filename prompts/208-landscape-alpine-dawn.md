# 208 — Landscape Alpine Dawn

Finalidade: paisagem fotográfica com lente e profundidade de campo real.

## Prompt (copiar)

Wide landscape photograph of a still alpine lake at dawn, thin mist drifting over the water, pine-covered slopes and jagged granite peaks reflecting in the surface, first pink and amber sunlight touching the highest ridge, a small wooden rowboat moored near a rocky shore in the foreground. 24mm f/8 lens, deep depth of field, crisp rock texture, dew on grass.

## Por que funciona

Lente wide + f/8 (profundidade) + detalhes de textura (dew, crisp rock) + luz específica. Frases completas para Flux.

# 416 — Over the Shoulder Glance

Finalidade: pose elegante com mistério e conexão visual, ideal para fashion e lifestyle.

## Prompt (copiar)

Photorealistic portrait of an adult model turned slightly away from the camera, body at three-quarter angle, looking back over the shoulder with a gentle smile and direct eye contact, one hand lightly touching the collarbone or shoulder, wearing elegant clothing, soft natural window light from the side, blurred outdoor or studio background, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, eye-level, natural skin texture with subtle imperfections, photorealistic.

## Por que funciona

O olhar por cima do ombro cria linha diagonal e intriga. O toque leve dá função à mão e evita braços pendurados.

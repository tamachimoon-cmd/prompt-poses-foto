# 255 — Walking mid-stride street

Finalidade: pose de caminhada natural em rua, capturando o momento da passada.

## Prompt (copiar)

Photorealistic full-body street photo of an adult walking across a quiet plaza, front foot just touching the ground, back heel lifted, arms swinging naturally opposite to the legs, head upright gazing ahead, slight side angle, soft daylight, 35mm lens, both shoes visible, space in front of the subject, natural skin texture.

## Por que funciona

Descrever o momento exato da passada (frente tocando, calcanhar levantado) força anatomia correta e movimento real, não freeze pose.

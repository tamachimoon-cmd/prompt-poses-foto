# 387 — Low Angle Power Pose

Finalidade: pose de poder em ângulo baixo para elongar e projetar presença heroica.

## Prompt (copiar)

Photorealistic full-body photograph of a confident adult woman standing with hands in pockets or one hand on hip, strong posture, chin slightly down, eyes looking into the lens, shot from a dramatic low angle near knee height tilted upward, modern city sidewalk or open outdoor space with skyscrapers or open sky behind, golden hour or strong natural rim light, 24mm lens at f/2.8, cinematic contrast, photorealistic skin and fabric texture, both feet fully visible, powerful editorial street style.

## Por que funciona

Ângulo baixo alonga as pernas e aumenta a estatura visual. Mãos nos bolsos ou no quadril reforçam o controle. Lente wide controlada cria drama sem distorção extrema.

# 227 — Seated forward lean engagement

Finalidade: pose sentada engajada e confiante para entrevistas, headshots ou conteúdo profissional.

## Prompt (copiar)

Seated portrait of an adult model leaning slightly forward toward the camera, elbows resting lightly on thighs or table, hands relaxed and visible, open confident expression with direct eye contact, shoulders dropped, 85mm lens at f/2.0, soft three-point lighting or large softbox, clean neutral background, camera at eye level, sharp eyes with catchlights, natural skin texture, professional yet approachable mood, photorealistic studio photography, both hands with clear function.

## Por que funciona

Inclinar-se para frente cria conexão e energia. Cotovelos e mãos com função evitam braços mortos. Olhar direto aumenta engajamento.

# 225 — Window-light kitchen portrait

Finalidade: retrato ambiental intimista com luz de janela e textura real de pele, ideal para lifestyle ou editorial cotidiano.

## Prompt (copiar)

Portrait of an adult woman in her sixties leaning on a kitchen counter with a cup of coffee, soft overcast light from a window on the left, visible pores and fine lines, a few loose gray hairs, everyday clutter blurred in the background, 85mm lens at f/2, eye level, natural colors, unretouched, natural skin texture, photorealistic.

## Por que funciona

Luz de janela nomeada com direção + lente 85mm + imperfeições explícitas (poros, fios soltos, unretouched) destroem o look plástico de IA. Clutter no fundo aumenta autenticidade.

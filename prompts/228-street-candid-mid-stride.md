# 228 — Street candid mid-stride

Finalidade: retrato de rua candid com movimento natural para street photography ou lifestyle urbano.

## Prompt (copiar)

Candid street photograph of an adult model mid-stride walking on a city sidewalk, natural posture with one foot forward, arms swinging naturally, slight motion blur on the feet if needed, looking slightly off-camera or toward the lens with relaxed expression, 35mm lens, available light or golden hour, shallow depth of field with blurred urban background, Kodak Portra 400 film grain aesthetic, true-to-life skin and clothing texture, documentary street photography style, authentic unposed energy.

## Por que funciona

Passo no meio da passada transmite vida. 35mm captura contexto sem distorcer demais. Grão de filme e luz disponível reforçam autenticidade.

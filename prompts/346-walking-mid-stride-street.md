# 346 — Walking mid stride street

Finalidade: passo no meio da passada em rua, movimento natural.

## Prompt (copiar)

Full-body street-style photograph of an adult model walking across a quiet pedestrian plaza, captured at the moment the front foot has just touched the ground and the back heel is lifted, arms swinging gently opposite to the legs, head upright, gaze ahead, slight side angle, whole body and both shoes visible, space in front of the subject, soft daylight, simple background, 35mm lens, natural skin texture, photorealistic documentary style.

## Por que funciona

Momento específico do passo + braços opostos criam movimento legível. Espaço à frente evita corte e dá direção.

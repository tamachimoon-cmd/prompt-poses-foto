# 231 — Arms crossed studio power

Finalidade: pose de confiança em estúdio com braços cruzados.

## Prompt (copiar)

Studio portrait of an adult model standing with arms crossed in front of the chest, elbows slightly bent, direct confident gaze into the camera, soft even studio lighting or softbox from the side, clean neutral backdrop, 85mm lens at f/2.8, three-quarter or waist-up framing, natural skin texture, strong but approachable expression, professional commercial or LinkedIn style photography.

## Por que funciona

Braços cruzados comunicam autoridade de forma clara e são anatomicamente simples para a IA. Softbox lateral adiciona volume sem drama excessivo.

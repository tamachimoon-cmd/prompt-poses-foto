# Retrato cândido fotorrealista (35mm)

## Uso

Foto que parece capturada no momento, não posada: textura real de pele, lente 50mm, plano médio na altura dos olhos, sem retoque pesado.

## Prompt

```text
Create a photorealistic candid photograph of an elderly sailor standing on a small fishing boat. 
He has weathered skin with visible wrinkles, pores, and sun texture, and a few faded traditional sailor tattoos on his arms. 
He is calmly adjusting a net while his dog sits nearby on the deck. Shot like a 35mm film photograph, medium close-up at eye level, using a 50mm lens. 
Soft coastal daylight, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, natural color balance. 
The image should feel honest and unposed, with real skin texture, worn materials, and everyday detail. No glamorization, no heavy retouching.
```

## Fonte

- OpenAI Cookbook — Gpt-image-1.5 Prompting Guide
- https://developers.openai.com/cookbook/examples/multimodal/image-gen-1.5-prompting_guide

## Dicas

A fonte recomenda incluir a palavra 'photorealistic' e evitar termos de polimento de estúdio. Use quality="high" quando o detalhe importa. O mesmo prompt aparece no guia oficial de image prompting da OpenAI.

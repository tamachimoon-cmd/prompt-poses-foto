Editorial color portrait of an adult model in her late 20s seated sideways on a wooden stool, waist-up framing, near elbow resting on the knee and fingertips lightly touching the jaw, shoulders relaxed, direct calm gaze, wearing a cobalt blue tailored blazer and matching wide trousers against a seamless burnt-orange paper backdrop, complementary blue and orange palette with the orange background slightly desaturated so the blue wardrobe stays dominant, skin tones kept natural and neutral, large octabox key camera-right with soft fill, 85mm lens at f/4, sharp eyes, clean fabric texture, polished fashion editorial, no colored gels on the face, no oversaturation, no additional accent colors.

## Por que funciona

Cores opostas no círculo cromático (azul e laranja) criam contraste forte sem precisar de luz dramática. Deixar uma cor dominante e a outra levemente dessaturada evita choque visual. Proibir gel no rosto preserva a pele natural.

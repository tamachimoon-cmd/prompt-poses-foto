# 317 — Natural Walking Mid-Stride

Finalidade: movimento natural de rua, full-body com energia controlada.

## Prompt (copiar)

Full-body street-style photo of an adult model walking across a quiet plaza. Capture the moment the front foot just touches the ground and the back heel is lifted. Arms swing gently opposite to the legs. Head upright, gaze ahead. Slight side angle, whole body and both shoes visible, space in front. Soft daylight, simple background, realistic photography, clean hands and feet.

## Por que funciona

Momento específico da passada + braços opostos cria movimento crível sem motion blur excessivo.

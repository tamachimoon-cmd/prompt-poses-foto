# 273 — Rembrandt com rebatedor de preenchimento

Finalidade: retrato Rembrandt legível para uso comercial, sombra com detalhe e triângulo preservado.

Fórmula: key 45° alto + rebatedor oposto ao nível dos olhos + razão 3:1 a 4:1 + 105mm + textura + restrições

## Prompt (copiar)

Head-and-shoulders studio portrait of an adult man in his early 40s seated on a stool, shoulders turned 30 degrees to camera-right, face turned back toward camera so the far cheek faces the key, chin level, relaxed mouth, steady gaze into the lens, dark green crew-neck sweater, mid-grey backdrop, single medium softbox key about 45 degrees to camera-left and roughly 60 cm above eye level, angled down so the nose and brow shadows leave a small triangle of light under the shadow-side eye, about the width of the eye, white reflector on the opposite side at eye level about one meter from his face returning soft fill so the shadow side keeps detail at roughly a 3:1 to 4:1 ratio, 105mm lens at f/5.6, sharp eyes with one catchlight each, natural skin texture with pores and fine lines, both hands complete with five natural fingers each, natural joint angles, realistic anatomy if hands enter the frame, no flat frontal fill, no plastic skin.

## Por que funciona

Sem fill o Rembrandt vai a 8:1 ou mais e a sombra some; o rebatedor oposto ao key, na altura dos olhos, leva a razão para cerca de 3:1–4:1, mantendo o triângulo e o detalhe. O triângulo deve ter mais ou menos o tamanho do olho: maior que isso já vira loop. A luz alta e inclinada é o que forma o limite superior do triângulo.

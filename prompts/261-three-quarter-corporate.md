# 261 — Three-quarter corporate headshot

Finalidade: headshot profissional de LinkedIn ou site.

Fórmula: three-quarter view + softbox + fundo neutro.

## Prompt (copiar)

Corporate headshot of an adult model in a navy blazer over white shirt, three-quarter view with shoulders angled, slight head tilt, direct eye contact and confident natural smile, large softbox key camera-left, subtle fill, clean light grey seamless background, 85mm lens at f/2.8, sharp eyes, natural skin texture, professional photography, polished and approachable.

## Por que funciona

Three-quarter é o ângulo mais favorecedor para a maioria dos rostos. Softbox + fundo neutro entrega o look corporativo limpo que o modelo reproduz com alta fidelidade.

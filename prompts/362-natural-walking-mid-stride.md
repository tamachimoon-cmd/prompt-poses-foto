# 362 — Natural Walking Mid-Stride

Finalidade: movimento natural de caminhada para street e lifestyle, capturando energia sem blur excessivo.

## Prompt (copiar)

Full-body street-style photo of an adult model walking across a quiet pedestrian plaza in casual outfit. Capture the moment when the front foot has just touched the ground and the back heel is lifted. Let the arms swing gently in opposite directions to the legs. Keep the head upright and gaze ahead. Photograph from a slight side angle with the whole body and both shoes visible. Leave space in front of the subject. Soft daylight and a simple background, 35mm lens at f/2.8, realistic photography style, no motion blur.

## Por que funciona

Momento específico (pé da frente no chão, calcanhar de trás levantado) dá o passo natural. Braços em oposição evitam pose estática. Espaço à frente melhora a composição de movimento.

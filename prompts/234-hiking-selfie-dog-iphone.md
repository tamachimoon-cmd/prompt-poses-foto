# 234 — Hiking selfie with dog iPhone

Finalidade: selfie casual de trilha com animal, estilo smartphone overexposed.

## Prompt (copiar)

Casual selfie-style photo of a young adult man with a golden retriever on a hiking trail, slightly overexposed sky, shot on iPhone, natural skin texture, wind in hair, relaxed pose, handheld framing, photorealistic unretouched look.

## Por que funciona

iPhone + sky overexposed + wind + handheld quebram a perfeição de estúdio. Textura natural de pele e animal aumenta realismo.

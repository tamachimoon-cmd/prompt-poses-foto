# 222 — Rembrandt studio headshot

Finalidade: headshot clássico de estúdio com iluminação Rembrandt para look profissional ou LinkedIn/editorial.

## Prompt (copiar)

Photorealistic headshot of an adult model, medium close-up, Rembrandt lighting with key light at 45 degrees creating a triangle of light on the far cheek, soft fill from the opposite side, neutral gray seamless backdrop, 85mm lens at f/1.8, camera at eye level, sharp focus on eyes with natural catchlights, visible skin pores and subtle texture, confident calm expression, shoulders slightly angled, natural neck length, no over-smoothing, professional commercial photography style.

## Por que funciona

Rembrandt lighting adiciona dimensão e drama controlado sem parecer artificial. 85mm comprime levemente e lisonjeia o rosto. Textura de pele explícita evita o look plástico da IA.

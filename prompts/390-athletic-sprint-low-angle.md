# 390 — Athletic Sprint Low Angle

Finalidade: pose atlética de sprint em ângulo baixo para energia e drama de movimento.

## Prompt (copiar)

Photorealistic full-body photograph of an athletic adult woman in streetwear sprinting mid-stride, intent gaze forward, arms pumping naturally, full body from low angle, urban crosswalk or open space at dusk, motion blur on edges and background, cinematic rim light, 35mm lens, rule of thirds composition, true-to-life athletic proportions, natural hand and foot anatomy, high-energy sports lifestyle style.

## Por que funciona

Ângulo baixo + mid-stride maximiza dinamismo. Gaze forward direciona a energia. Motion blur controlado sugere velocidade sem perder o sujeito.

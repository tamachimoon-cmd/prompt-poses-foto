# 233 — Ceramic artist studio environmental

Finalidade: retrato ambiental de trabalho com mãos sujas e luz industrial.

## Prompt (copiar)

Environmental portrait of an adult ceramic artist in her cluttered studio, hands covered in clay, overcast light through large industrial windows, 35mm f/2, muted colors, natural skin and fabric texture, working posture, photorealistic documentary style.

## Por que funciona

35mm f/2 captura contexto do estúdio; argila nas mãos + luz overcast industrial + cores muted dão autenticidade de processo real.

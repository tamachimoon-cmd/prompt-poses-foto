# 340 — Golden hour environmental

Finalidade: retrato ambiental com luz dourada e fundo narrativo.

## Prompt (copiar)

Environmental portrait of an adult model standing near a wooden dock at golden hour, warm side light raking across the face and creating long shadows, weathered wooden boats and calm water in soft bokeh behind, 50mm lens at f/2.8, natural skin texture with subtle pores, relaxed weight shift on one leg, hands loosely at sides, slight film grain, Kodak Portra 400 color science, candid documentary feel.

## Por que funciona

Luz lateral + bokeh de fundo + filme Portra empurram para fotografia real. Peso deslocado evita a pose estática de manequim.

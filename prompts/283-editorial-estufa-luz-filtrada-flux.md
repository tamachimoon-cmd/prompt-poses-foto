# 283 — Editorial em estufa com luz filtrada (Flux)

Finalidade: editorial de moda em linguagem natural para Flux, com padrões de sombra da luz.

Fórmula: meio/estilo + sujeito e ação + ambiente + luz e câmera em frases completas + restrições

## Prompt (copiar)

An editorial fashion photograph of an adult model in her early 30s with striking cheekbones and slicked-back hair, wearing an oversized white linen shirt tucked into wide charcoal trousers, standing in an abandoned greenhouse with shattered glass ceiling panels. She stands with her weight on the left leg, the right knee softly bent and turned inward, her left hand resting in her pocket with the thumb outside and her right hand lightly touching a rusted iron column at shoulder height, her head turned toward the camera with a calm, unsmiling gaze. Golden hour light streams through the broken glass, creating dramatic shadow patterns across the floor and her shirt while her face sits in a clean patch of light. Vogue magazine aesthetic, shot on a medium format camera with an 80mm lens, rich contrast with lifted blacks, natural skin texture with visible pores, both hands complete with five natural fingers each, natural joint angles, realistic anatomy, no plastic skin.

## Por que funciona

Flux entende frases completas e relações espaciais, então descrever a pose em sentenças ("weight on the left leg", "hand touching the column") funciona melhor que palavras soltas. A base vem do exemplo avançado da fonte: estilo, sujeito, ambiente e detalhes técnicos. Pedir uma janela de luz limpa no rosto evita que o padrão de sombra corte os olhos.

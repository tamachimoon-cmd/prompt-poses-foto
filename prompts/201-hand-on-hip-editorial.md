# 201 — Hand on hip editorial

Finalidade: pose de poder editorial para campanhas de moda ou retratos de impacto.

## Prompt (copiar)

Full-body editorial photograph of an adult male model in his mid-30s in a minimal studio, one hand firmly on the hip with elbow out, the other hand raised lightly touching the side of the neck, hip shifted to one side, one knee softly bent, chin lifted with intense gaze to camera, wearing a structured black coat and tailored trousers, dramatic side lighting with soft falloff, dark gradient background, 85mm lens at f/2.8, camera at eye level, rich fabric texture, natural skin pores, photorealistic high-fashion magazine style, no plastic skin.

## Por que funciona

A combinação de mão no quadril e no pescoço cria atitude e linha diagonal. O deslocamento do quadril adiciona dinamismo sem perder a visibilidade da roupa.

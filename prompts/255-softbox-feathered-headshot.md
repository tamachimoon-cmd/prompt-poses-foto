# 255 — Headshot softbox feathered

Finalidade: retrato profissional limpo e favorecedor.

Fórmula: softbox grande feathered + fill controlado + 85mm.

## Prompt (copiar)

Professional head-and-shoulders portrait of an adult model wearing a light blue collared shirt, slight head tilt, direct eye contact with natural approachable smile, large softbox key light camera-left feathered so the light wraps softly across the face, subtle fill from camera-right reflector, faint rim on the hair, neutral grey seamless background, 85mm lens at f/2.8, sharp eyes, natural skin texture, soft catchlights, clean corporate photography, no harsh shadows under the chin.

## Por que funciona

“Feathered” e posição exata do softbox produzem transição suave que modelos fotorealistas entendem melhor do que “soft lighting”.

# 225 — Relaxed three-quarter portrait

Finalidade: retrato editorial de três quartos relaxado, ideal para lifestyle ou perfil profissional com naturalidade.

## Prompt (copiar)

Create a realistic editorial portrait of an adult person wearing a rust-colored work jacket and dark trousers. They stand with their weight on the back leg and their torso turned slightly away from the camera. Their head turns gently toward the lens, chin level and shoulders relaxed. Both arms hang naturally with loose hands visible beside the hips. Frame from mid-thigh upward at eye level. Plain warm gray background, soft window light from the left, natural expression and skin texture, photorealistic, 85mm lens.

## Por que funciona

Deslocamento de peso + torso angulado cria dinamismo sem tensão. Mãos soltas e visíveis evitam problemas de anatomia. Luz de janela + fundo neutro dá controle profissional.

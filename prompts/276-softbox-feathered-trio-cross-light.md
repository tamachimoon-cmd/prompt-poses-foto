# 276 — Trio em cross-light com softboxes feathered

Finalidade: retrato de equipe pequena ou família adulta com luz uniforme em todos os rostos.

Fórmula: duas softboxes cruzadas + feathering (borda do feixe) + luzes recuadas + 50mm f/8 + restrições

## Prompt (copiar)

Group portrait of three adult colleagues in their 30s and 40s standing in a staggered line in a white studio, the middle person half a step forward, the two outer people angled 30 degrees toward the center, all with weight on the back leg, one with hands loosely clasped in front, one with a hand in a trouser pocket thumb out, one holding a closed notebook at waist height, all looking into the lens with relaxed smiles, smart-casual clothing in navy, grey and cream, two large softboxes placed on opposite sides of the group, crossing their beams in front of the subjects and feathered slightly away so only the soft edge of each beam reaches the faces, lights set back several meters for even coverage, balanced exposure from left to right, no face falling into a neighbor's shadow, 50mm lens at f/8 so all three faces are sharp, natural skin texture on every face, both hands complete with five natural fingers each, natural joint angles, realistic anatomy for every person, no duplicated faces, no merged limbs, no plastic skin.

## Por que funciona

Em grupo, a luz direta deixa o centro estourado e as bordas escuras; cruzar duas softboxes e usar a borda do feixe (feathering) iguala a exposição. Recuar as luzes, pela lei do inverso do quadrado, reduz a queda de luz de um lado ao outro. f/8 garante foco em todos os rostos.

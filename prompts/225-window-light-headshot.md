# 225 — Window light headshot

Finalidade: headshot natural com luz de janela para look profissional, realista e lisonjeiro.

## Prompt (copiar)

Photorealistic professional headshot of an adult model, chest up, facing camera with a relaxed half-smile. Soft natural window light from the left, gentle falloff into shadow on the right cheek, no artificial fill. 85mm lens at f/1.8, shallow depth of field, background softly blurred neutral gray. Warm natural skin tones, fine grain, visible skin texture with pores, sharp eyes with catchlights, photorealistic, no retouching gloss, natural proportions.

## Por que funciona

Luz de janela é a mais lisonjeira e realista. 85mm + f/1.8 isola o sujeito e comprime o fundo. Cláusula de textura de pele evita o look plástico.

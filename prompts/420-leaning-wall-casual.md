# 420 — Leaning Against Wall Casual

Finalidade: pose descontraída e cool para lifestyle urbano ou casual editorial.

## Prompt (copiar)

Photorealistic full-body photograph of an adult model leaning casually against a wall or surface, one leg bent with foot against the wall, hands in pockets or one hand in pocket, relaxed confident expression looking at camera, wearing casual urban clothing, rustic or city alley background, natural daylight with soft shadows, 35mm lens, eye-level, natural skin texture, both feet visible, photorealistic.

## Por que funciona

O apoio na parede + perna dobrada cria linhas interessantes e sensação de effortless. Mãos nos bolsos resolvem o problema de mãos soltas.

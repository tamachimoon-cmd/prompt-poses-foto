# 345 — Butterfly beauty closeup

Finalidade: close de beleza com iluminação butterfly.

## Prompt (copiar)

Extreme beauty close-up of an adult model, butterfly lighting from directly above with a soft reflector under the chin, subtle catchlights, visible skin texture with freckles and pores, soft natural makeup, clean light gray background, 100mm macro lens, sharp focus on eyes, no plastic smoothing, commercial beauty photography style, photorealistic.

## Por que funciona

Butterfly + reflector controla sombras sob o queixo e olhos. Macro + textura de pele são o antídoto direto ao look CGI.

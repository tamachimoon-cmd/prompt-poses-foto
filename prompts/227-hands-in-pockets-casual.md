# 227 — Hands in pockets casual lean

Finalidade: pose casual relaxada de corpo inteiro ou 3/4 com mãos nos bolsos.

## Prompt (copiar)

Full-length or three-quarter outdoor photograph of an adult model standing with both hands in front pockets, weight shifted onto one leg, relaxed shoulders, slight lean, looking at camera with a calm confident expression, city street or park background softly blurred, natural daylight, 35mm or 50mm lens at f/2.8, realistic fabric texture on clothing, natural skin texture, candid lifestyle photography, no stiff posture.

## Por que funciona

Mãos nos bolsos resolvem o problema clássico de dedos deformados da IA e ainda passam relaxamento. Peso deslocado evita a postura de manequim.

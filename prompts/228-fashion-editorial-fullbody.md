# 228 — Fashion editorial full body

## Prompt (copiar)

Fashion editorial photograph of an adult model wearing tailored outfit, striking contrapposto pose with weight on one leg, looking over the shoulder with intense gaze. Full-length framing, hard beauty-dish light, crisp shadows, against seamless color backdrop. 70mm lens, magazine cover quality, natural skin texture, elegant hands with clear function, photorealistic, high fashion style.

## Por que funciona

Contrapposto + olhar sobre o ombro cria dinamismo. Beauty dish dá o look editorial clássico. Enquadramento full body mostra o figurino.

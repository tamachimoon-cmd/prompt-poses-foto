# 223 — Golden hour environmental portrait

Finalidade: retrato ambiental outdoor com luz dourada para lifestyle, branding ou editorial natural.

## Prompt (copiar)

Environmental portrait of an adult model outdoors at golden hour, warm side light raking across the face with soft rim light on hair and shoulders, relaxed natural posture with weight on one leg, one hand lightly resting at the side, looking slightly off-camera with a soft expression, 50mm lens at f/2.0, shallow depth of field with creamy bokeh background of trees or street, true-to-life skin texture with warm highlights, photorealistic documentary style, both feet visible if full body, natural hands.

## Por que funciona

Golden hour entrega luz quente e separação automática. Postura com peso deslocado evita rigidez. 50mm inclui mais contexto sem distorcer.

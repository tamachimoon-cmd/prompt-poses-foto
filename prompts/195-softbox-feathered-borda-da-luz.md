## Prompt (copiar)

Waist-up studio portrait of an adult model in her mid-30s, shoulders turned three-quarters, hands loosely clasped at waist height, face turned back toward the key light, wearing a deep burgundy silk blouse, single 90x60cm rectangular softbox placed camera-left slightly above eye level and feathered: rotated so its center points just in front of her face and toward the camera, lighting her with the edge of the beam, creating a gradual falloff from the lit cheek across to the far side of the face and down the torso, the light angled away from the grey backdrop so the background renders as a darker charcoal vignette, white fill card on camera-right feathered toward her face only, 85mm lens at f/4, sharp eyes, catchlight in the upper part of the eyes, natural skin texture, no flat frontal light, no hot spot on the forehead.

## Por que funciona

Feathering é girar a softbox para que o sujeito receba a borda do feixe, não o centro: dá transição gradual e controla o spill. Apontar a luz para longe do fundo escurece o cenário sem mexer no tripé. A fonte lembra que feathering não deixa a luz mais macia por si só; a maciez vem do tamanho da fonte.

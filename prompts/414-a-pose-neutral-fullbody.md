# 414 — A-Pose Neutral Full Body

Finalidade: pose de referência neutra e anatômica para base de modelos ou catálogo, sem drama.

## Prompt (copiar)

Photorealistic full-body studio photograph of an adult model standing straight in a neutral A-pose, feet hip-width apart, arms straight and angled down and away from the body at about 45 degrees, palms facing the thighs, shoulders relaxed, head level looking straight at the camera, wearing simple fitted neutral clothing, plain light grey seamless background, soft even studio lighting from front, 50mm lens, eye-level camera, natural skin texture, both hands and feet fully visible, no locked joints, sharp focus, high detail.

## Por que funciona

A-pose é a base anatômica mais clara para IA e ControlNet. Descrição precisa de ângulos dos braços evita braços colados ou T-pose rígido.

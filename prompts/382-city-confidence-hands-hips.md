# 382 — City Confidence Hands on Hips

Finalidade: pose de confiança urbana com mãos na cintura para retratos profissionais ou lifestyle.

## Prompt (copiar)

Photorealistic full-body photograph of a confident adult woman standing with hands on her hips, elbows slightly bent outward, shoulders back, looking straight at the camera with direct gaze, against a modern cityscape backdrop with soft bokeh, realistic photography style, natural daylight with mild rim light, 35mm lens at f/4, eye-level to slightly low angle, true-to-life proportions, both hands with clear finger definition, urban professional mood.

## Por que funciona

Mãos na cintura abrem o torso e projetam autoridade. Contato visual direto cria conexão. Cityscape dá contexto sem dominar.

# 541 — Seated on Stool Forward Lean

Finalidade: pose sentada de corpo inteiro em banco, com contato claro das mãos e pés.

## Prompt (copiar)

Create a full-body studio portrait of an adult model wearing a rust-colored work jacket and dark trousers seated near the front of a simple wooden stool. Both feet rest flat on the floor, one slightly ahead of the other. The torso leans forward a little from the hips. Rest each hand loosely on its corresponding thigh, with fingers relaxed and visible. Keep the head upright and gaze toward the camera. Include the entire stool and both shoes, with space below the feet. Soft side lighting and a neutral backdrop, photorealistic editorial.

## Por que funciona

Pontos de contato (pés no chão, mãos nas coxas) dão estabilidade à IA. Incluir banco e sapatos inteiros evita cortes ruins.

# 332 — Hands on hips city confidence

Finalidade: pose de confiança com mãos no quadril contra paisagem urbana.

## Prompt (copiar)

Waist-up or full-body photograph of an adult standing with hands on hips, elbows slightly bent and relaxed, direct confident gaze at camera, slight weight shift, against a cityscape or urban backdrop, soft natural light, shot on 50mm lens, natural skin texture, realistic fingers and posture, photorealistic lifestyle or business portrait.

## Por que funciona

Mãos no quadril comunicam auto-confiança sem agressividade. Weight shift + elbows relaxed evitam rigidez.

# 250 — Retrato de maturidade com textura de rugas

Finalidade: retrato documental de pessoa idosa, celebrando textura de pele e idade.

## Prompt (copiar)

Photorealistic head-and-shoulders portrait of an adult man in his late sixties with detailed wrinkles and weathered skin texture, short grey beard, wearing a navy knit cardigan over a collared shirt, seated with shoulders turned slightly to camera-right, face toward the lens with a calm, warm expression, natural window light from camera-right creating soft shadows on the far side of the face, dark warm-grey background, 85mm lens at f/2.0, sharp focus on the eyes showing detailed iris texture, visible skin pores and age spots, warm neutral color grading, professional documentary photography style, 4:5 aspect ratio, avoid over-smoothing or artificial enhancement, no plastic skin, no wax-figure look.

## Por que funciona

Pele madura é onde o "plástico" aparece primeiro; pedir rugas, manchas de idade e íris detalhada mantém a pessoa real. Luz de janela lateral modela as rugas com sombras suaves em vez de achatá-las. O pedido explícito de evitar suavização segue o modelo de prompt da fonte.

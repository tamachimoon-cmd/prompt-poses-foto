# 489 — Window Light Portrait 85mm

Finalidade: retrato natural com luz de janela e compressão de lente.

## Prompt (copiar)

Photoreal portrait of an adult with relaxed expression, looking slightly off camera, natural skin texture with subtle imperfections, soft window light from the left, gentle shadow falloff, shallow depth of field, 85mm portrait photo, neutral unmarked background, high detail, realistic color, hands outside the composition

## Por que funciona

Luz de janela + 85mm + textura de pele realista produz retrato profissional e crível.

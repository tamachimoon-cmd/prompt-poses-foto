# 233 — Walking mid-stride candid

Finalidade: captura candid de movimento natural na rua ou ambiente externo.

## Prompt (copiar)

Candid street photograph of an adult model walking mid-stride toward or across the camera, natural arm swing, looking slightly to the side or ahead, urban street or park background with mild motion blur in the background, natural daylight or overcast, 35mm lens at f/2.8, documentary style, realistic clothing movement and skin texture, authentic unposed energy, Kodak Portra or Fujifilm film look.

## Por que funciona

Mid-stride + olhar não frontal mata a pose de catálogo. 35mm + motion blur no fundo vende "foto de verdade" em vez de render estático.

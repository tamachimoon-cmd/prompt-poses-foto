# 540 — Relaxed Three-Quarter Weight Shift

Finalidade: retrato editorial de três quartos com peso deslocado, natural e sem rigidez.

## Prompt (copiar)

Create a realistic editorial portrait of an adult model wearing a rust-colored work jacket and dark trousers. They stand with their weight on the back leg and their torso turned slightly away from the camera. Their head turns gently toward the lens, chin level and shoulders relaxed. Both arms hang naturally with loose hands visible beside the hips. Frame from mid-thigh upward at eye level. Plain warm gray background, soft window light from the left, natural expression and skin texture, photorealistic, sharp details.

## Por que funciona

Peso no perna traseira + torso em três quartos cria assimetria natural. Mãos soltas e visíveis evitam problemas de anatomia. Luz de janela suave mantém realismo.

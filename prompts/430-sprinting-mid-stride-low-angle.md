# 430 — Sprinting Mid-Stride Low Angle

Athletic adult model in streetwear, sprinting mid-stride, full-body from low angle, intent gaze forward, urban crosswalk at dusk, motion blur edges, cinematic rim light, 35mm lens, rule of thirds, photorealistic action photography --ar 16:9

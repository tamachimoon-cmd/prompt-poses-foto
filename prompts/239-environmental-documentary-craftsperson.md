# 239 — Environmental Documentary Craftsperson

Finalidade: retrato ambiental documental de profissional no local de trabalho.

## Prompt (copiar)

Environmental documentary portrait of an adult craftsperson in their workshop, weathered hands with visible veins and calluses resting on a workbench, deep facial creases, soft diffused natural light from a large north-facing window, muted earth tones, 50mm lens at f/4, rule of thirds, authentic and candid, photorealistic skin texture, documentary style.

## Por que funciona

Detalhes de mãos e rugas + luz de janela norte vendem autenticidade; enquadramento ambiental conta a história sem posar artificial.

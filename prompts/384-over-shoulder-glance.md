# 384 — Over Shoulder Glance

Finalidade: pose de olhar sobre o ombro para mistério e conexão em retratos lifestyle ou moda.

## Prompt (copiar)

Photorealistic three-quarter shot of an adult woman turned slightly away from the camera, looking back over her shoulder with a gentle smile, body weight on the back leg, one hand relaxed near the hip, in a sunlit outdoor setting with soft background bokeh, realistic photography style, natural side light with subtle rim, 70mm lens at f/2.5, camera at chest height, natural hand and facial anatomy, elegant lifestyle mood.

## Por que funciona

O olhar sobre o ombro cria dinamismo e curiosidade. O sorriso suave equilibra o mistério. Luz lateral esculpe o rosto e o ombro.

# 234 — Moody contemplation close-up

Finalidade: close-up introspectivo com olhar desviado para mood emocional.

## Prompt (copiar)

A close-up of a woman looking away from the camera, with a contemplative expression, head slightly tilted, soft features, in a dimly lit room with side lighting creating gentle shadows, realistic photography style, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, natural skin texture with subtle pores, photorealistic.

## Por que funciona

Olhar desviado + luz baixa cria mistério e profundidade. Close-up foca na expressão sem complicar o corpo.

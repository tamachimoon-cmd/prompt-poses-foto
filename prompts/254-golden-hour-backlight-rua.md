# 254 — Golden hour backlight na rua

Finalidade: lifestyle outdoor com rim quente e atmosfera.

Fórmula: backlight de golden hour + pose relaxada + lente 50mm.

## Prompt (copiar)

Outdoor lifestyle photograph of an adult model walking on a city street at golden hour, weight on the back foot mid-stride, arms relaxed at sides with slight natural swing, looking slightly off-camera to the right with a calm expression, warm amber backlight from low sun creating strong rim light on hair and shoulders, soft fill from ambient bounce, long gentle shadows, 50mm lens at f/2.0, shallow depth of field with creamy bokeh on background buildings, natural skin tones, photorealistic, no overexposure on face.

## Por que funciona

Golden hour com direção e qualidade descritas (low sun, rim, long shadows) entrega calor e separação sem pedir “sunset” vago. Pose de passada evita corpo rígido.

# 336 — Candid street rain 35mm

Finalidade: foto candid de rua com chuva, motion blur controlado e textura realista.

## Prompt (copiar)

Candid street photograph of a person in a dark rain jacket waiting at a wet crosswalk on a gray afternoon, glancing at their phone, city bus passing behind with slight motion blur, wet asphalt reflections, overcast soft daylight, shot on 35mm lens at eye level, slightly off-center framing, muted colors, fine grain, natural skin texture, documentary photography style, no plastic skin.

## Por que funciona

35mm + framing off-center + motion blur no fundo criam sensação de momento real capturado, não posado. Reflexos no asfalto e grão fino ancoram no mundo físico.

# 247 — Contraluz com duas strips atrás e haze

Finalidade: retrato de alto contraste com recorte de luz nas bordas, para editorial dramático ou capa.

## Prompt (copiar)

High-contrast rim-lit portrait of an adult model in his early 30s in an empty dark studio filled with light haze, three-quarter framing, standing square to camera with the chin slightly lowered and a steady direct gaze, black tailored overcoat, two tall strip lights placed behind the subject on the left and right, angled toward his back, drawing bright clean edges along the shoulders, arms and hair, a faint glow in the haze around the silhouette, very low frontal fill from a black-flagged reflector so the face remains readable in deep shadow, 85mm lens at f/2.8, sharp eyes, real skin texture visible in the highlights along the cheekbone, natural fabric texture, fine grain, 4:5 aspect ratio, no plastic skin, no lens flare streaks, no colored gels.

## Por que funciona

Duas strips atrás desenham as bordas e o haze torna o feixe visível, criando separação forte sem luz frontal. A fonte lembra que luz plana esconde textura e que contraluz/rim "joga sombra de verdade". Um fill mínimo mantém o rosto legível sem perder o drama.

# 251 — Candid street crosswalk

Finalidade: fotografia de rua candid com movimento e imperfeição de momento.

## Prompt (copiar)

Candid street photograph of a man in a rain jacket waiting at a crosswalk on a gray afternoon, glancing at his phone, a city bus passing behind him as a slight motion blur, wet asphalt reflections, 35mm lens at eye level, slightly off-center framing, muted colors, fine grain, natural posture with weight on one leg.

## Por que funciona

Enquadramento off-center + motion blur + 35mm documentary simulam foto real capturada no momento, não pose de estúdio.

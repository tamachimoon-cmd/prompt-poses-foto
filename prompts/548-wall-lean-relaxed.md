# 548 — Wall Lean Relaxed

Finalidade: pose de encostar na parede para relaxamento natural.

## Prompt (copiar)

Adult model leaning against a textured plaster wall with one shoulder, arms loosely crossed behind her back, fitted casual outfit, sunlit outdoor terrace, hard noon light from above right, 85mm f/2 lens, natural skin texture, photorealistic, both feet visible.

## Por que funciona

Ponto de suporte (parede) simplifica a pose e adiciona linhas. Braços atrás evitam problemas de mãos.

# 361 — Seated on Stool Forward Lean

Finalidade: pose sentada natural de corpo inteiro ou meio corpo para editorial e retrato ambiental.

## Prompt (copiar)

Full-body studio portrait of an adult model wearing a work jacket and dark trousers seated near the front of a simple wooden stool. Both feet rest flat on the floor, one slightly ahead of the other. The torso leans forward a little from the hips. Rest each hand loosely on its corresponding thigh, with fingers relaxed and visible. Keep the head upright and gaze toward the camera. Include the entire stool and both shoes, with space below the feet. Soft side lighting and a neutral backdrop, 50mm lens, realistic skin texture.

## Por que funciona

Pontos de contato claros (mãos nas coxas, pés no chão) reduzem erros de anatomia. Inclinar o torso quebra a pose rígida de "sentado direito". Ótimo para mostrar figurino e personalidade.

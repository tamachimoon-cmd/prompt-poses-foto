# 313 — Contrapposto Fashion Editorial

Finalidade: pose clássica de moda que flui e valoriza o figurino.

## Prompt (copiar)

Full-length editorial photograph of an adult model in contrapposto stance, weight on one leg, opposite hip pushed out, shoulders counter-tilted, one hand in pocket, other arm relaxed, three-quarter angle, soft studio lighting with rim light, high-fashion magazine style, 85mm lens, clean background, realistic fabric drape and skin texture.

## Por que funciona

Contrapposto cria curva em S natural; o ângulo três-quartos valoriza o volume do corpo e da roupa.

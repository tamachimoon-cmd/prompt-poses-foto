# 538 — Wall Lean Relaxado

Finalidade: pose casual e cool com apoio na parede, ideal para street ou fashion.

## Prompt (copiar)

Full-body realistic photography of an adult person leaning casually against a textured wall with one shoulder, one knee bent with foot flat against the wall, hands relaxed, in a sunlit alley or street, natural light, 35mm lens, photorealistic, natural skin texture, editorial style, --ar 3:4

## Por que funciona

O apoio na parede dá ponto de contato e relaxa a postura. O joelho dobrado cria linhas interessantes.

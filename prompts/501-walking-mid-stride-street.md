# 501 — Walking Mid Stride Street

Finalidade: pose de caminhada natural em rua, estilo street.

## Prompt (copiar)

Create a full-body street-style photo of an adult walking across a quiet pedestrian plaza. Capture the moment when the front foot has just touched the ground and the back heel is lifted. Let the arms swing gently in opposite directions to the legs. Keep the head upright and gaze ahead. Photograph from a slight side angle with the whole body and both shoes visible. Leave space in front of the subject. Soft daylight and a simple background, 35mm lens.

## Por que funciona

Momento específico da passada + braços opostos + espaço na frente + full body geram movimento crível.

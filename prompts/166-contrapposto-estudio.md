# 166 — Contrapposto de estúdio

Finalidade: pose de corpo inteiro com peso deslocado, para editorial ou lookbook.

Fórmula: enquadramento + figurino + pose espacial + fundo + luz + lente + textura.

## Prompt (copiar)

Full-length studio photograph of an adult model in a tailored black blazer, straight trousers and loafers, standing front-on in relaxed contrapposto, weight on the right leg, left knee soft and angled in, hips rotated about 10 degrees left, shoulders square to camera, right hand resting on the hip bone, left arm hanging straight, chin level, gaze direct, clean grey seamless cyclorama, large octabox key camera-left, subtle rim light on the far shoulder, 85mm lens at f/2.8, sharp eyes, natural skin texture, visible fabric weave, editorial photography, no extra limbs, both hands complete.

## Por que funciona

Contrapposto dá curva em S sem pedir "pose fashion" genérica. Octabox a 45 graus esculpe o rosto; rim separa o ombro do fundo cinza.

# 231 — Freckled redhead window Portra

Finalidade: close-up de retrato com luz de janela e look de filme Portra.

## Prompt (copiar)

Close-up portrait of a freckled red-haired adult woman in a mustard sweater, looking slightly past the camera, soft window light from the right, shot on 85mm f/1.4, shallow depth of field, Kodak Portra 400 film look, natural skin texture with visible freckles and pores, photorealistic.

## Por que funciona

85mm f/1.4 + luz de janela + olhar past camera + Portra 400 + sardas/poros explícitos produzem pele real e bokeh natural.

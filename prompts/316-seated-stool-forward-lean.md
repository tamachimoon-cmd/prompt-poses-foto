# 316 — Seated Stool Forward Lean

Finalidade: pose sentada natural e engajada, boa para retrato de corpo inteiro ou editorial.

## Prompt (copiar)

Full-body studio portrait of an adult model wearing a simple jacket and dark trousers, seated near the front edge of a plain wooden stool. Both feet flat on the floor, one slightly ahead of the other. Torso leans forward gently from the hips. Each hand rests loosely on the corresponding thigh, fingers relaxed and visible. Head upright, gaze toward camera. Entire stool and both shoes visible, soft side lighting, neutral backdrop, realistic photography, natural anatomy.

## Por que funciona

Contatos claros (mãos nas coxas, pés no chão) e inclinação sutil evitam rigidez; enquadramento completo reduz cortes problemáticos.

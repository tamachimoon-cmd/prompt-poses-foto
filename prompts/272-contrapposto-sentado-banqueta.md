# 272 — Contrapposto sentado na banqueta

Finalidade: variação sentada do contrapposto para editorial de moda ou retrato de estúdio.

Fórmula: pose sentada assimétrica + borda do assento + luz suave lateral + 85mm + textura real + restrições anatômicas

## Prompt (copiar)

Three-quarter-length studio portrait of an adult model in her early 30s seated on the front third of a tall wooden stool, spine long, weight shifted onto her right hip so the shoulders tilt gently opposite to the hips, right foot on the floor with the knee soft, left foot resting on the stool's footrest a step higher, left forearm resting lightly on the raised left thigh, right hand loosely on the stool seat beside her, head turned about 20 degrees toward camera-left, chin slightly forward and down, calm direct gaze, camel knit dress with opaque tights and ankle boots, warm grey seamless backdrop, large octabox key camera-left at 45 degrees above eye level, white reflector camera-right, 85mm lens at f/4, sharp eyes, natural skin texture with visible pores, both hands complete with five natural fingers each, natural joint angles, realistic anatomy, every major joint softly bent, no slouching into the seat, no plastic skin, no beauty filter.

## Por que funciona

O contrapposto sentado nasce do mesmo princípio do em pé: peso num lado do quadril e ombros compensando para o outro. Sentar no terço da frente mantém a coluna longa e evita que o corpo afunde. Pés em alturas diferentes e articulações levemente dobradas ("se dobra, dobre") tiram o aspecto de manequim.

# 485 — Walking Mid-Stride Street

Finalidade: movimento natural de caminhada para street photography ou fashion.

## Prompt (copiar)

Full-body street-style photo of an adult walking across a quiet pedestrian plaza. Capture the moment when the front foot has just touched the ground and the back heel is lifted. Arms swing gently opposite to the legs. Head upright, gaze ahead. Slight side angle, whole body and both shoes visible. Soft daylight, simple background, 35mm lens, photorealistic --ar 2:3

## Por que funciona

Momento específico da passada + braços opostos gera movimento crível e evita pose estática.

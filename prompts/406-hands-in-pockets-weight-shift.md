# 406 — Hands in Pockets Weight Shift

Finalidade: pose casual descontraída com mãos nos bolsos e deslocamento de peso. Evita rigidez em retratos de rua ou lifestyle.

## Prompt (copiar)

Create a casual portrait of an adult person in a jacket and straight-leg trousers. The subject stands with feet comfortably apart, weight resting on one leg, and both hands partly tucked into the front trouser pockets with thumbs visible. Keep the elbows close to the body and shoulders lowered. Use a three-quarter composition that includes the knees, photographed at chest height. Quiet outdoor wall, soft afternoon light, relaxed expression, photorealistic, natural skin texture, 50mm lens, --ar 3:4

## Por que funciona

Polegares visíveis evitam mãos “sumindo” nos bolsos. Peso em uma perna + ombros baixos geram naturalidade real. Cotovelos próximos do corpo impedem pose de “manequim”.

# 232 — Yoga tree pose sunrise

Full-body eye-level shot of an adult woman in yoga vrikshasana tree pose, weight on one leg, opposite foot on inner thigh, hands in prayer or relaxed, serene expression, sunrise terrace, pastel haze, 35mm lens, centered composition, photorealistic soft light —ar 9:16

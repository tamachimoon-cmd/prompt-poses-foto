# 413 — Thoughtful Desk Portrait

Finalidade: retrato pensativo em mesa de trabalho, com olhar para a janela. Ideal para profissionais, escritores ou lifestyle intelectual.

## Prompt (copiar)

Create a waist-up portrait of an adult person seated at an uncluttered desk. Turn the torso slightly toward a nearby window. Rest the left forearm on the desk and place the right hand loosely beside a closed notebook. Keep both hands away from the face and clearly separated. The subject looks toward the window with a quiet expression. Photograph from a three-quarter angle at eye level, using natural window light and a softly blurred workspace. Photorealistic, natural skin texture, 50mm lens, --ar 3:4

## Por que funciona

Mãos com função clara (apoiada na mesa, ao lado do caderno) e separadas evitam problemas de anatomia. Olhar para a janela + luz natural cria introspecção crível.

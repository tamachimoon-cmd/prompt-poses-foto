# 275 — Golden hour lateral a 90° com fill dourado

Finalidade: retrato externo mais dramático e editorial, com metade do rosto na luz dourada.

Fórmula: sol lateral 90° + fill dourado no lado da sombra + 50mm + textura + restrições

## Prompt (copiar)

Waist-up outdoor editorial portrait of an adult man in his late 20s at golden hour, standing with his body angled 45 degrees to camera-right and the low sun exactly 90 degrees to his left, so warm side light illuminates the left half of his face while the right half falls into soft shadow, a gold reflector on the shadow side at chest height lifting the shadow just enough to keep the eye readable, weight on the right leg, left hand in the pocket of his rust-colored chore jacket with the thumb outside, right arm relaxed with the elbow slightly bent, head turned back toward the lens, slight confident half-smile, out-of-focus coastal dunes behind, 50mm lens at f/2, strong dimensional modelling of the cheekbone and jaw, natural skin texture with stubble and pores, both hands complete with five natural fingers each, natural joint angles, realistic anatomy, no blown highlights on the lit cheek, no plastic skin.

## Por que funciona

Sol a 90° cria modelagem forte, quase um split, que revela textura e forma. Um rebatedor dourado do lado da sombra suaviza o contraste e mantém a paleta quente da golden hour, como descreve a fonte. Mão no bolso com polegar para fora dá função ao braço.

# 329 — Seated cross-legged zen

Finalidade: pose sentada cross-legged para retrato de lifestyle, wellness ou indoor relaxado.

## Prompt (copiar)

Photorealistic portrait of an adult sitting cross-legged on a soft surface in a cozy indoor space, hands resting lightly on knees, relaxed and content expression, soft natural window light from the side, warm neutral tones, natural skin texture, shot on 50mm lens, eye-level camera, shallow depth of field, realistic hands and posture, photorealistic lifestyle photography.

## Por que funciona

Pose sentada com mãos apoiadas reduz tensão. Luz de janela + expressão relaxada transmite calma sem pose forçada.

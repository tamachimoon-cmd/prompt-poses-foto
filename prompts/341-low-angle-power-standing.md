# 341 — Low angle power standing

Finalidade: pose de poder com câmera baixa e 35mm.

## Prompt (copiar)

Full-body photograph of an adult model standing with weight on the back leg, hands on hips, elbows slightly bent, looking straight at camera with confident expression, shot from a low angle near knee height with 35mm lens, strong rim light from behind, urban concrete background, natural skin texture, sharp eyes, photorealistic, powerful but natural stance.

## Por que funciona

Ângulo baixo + 35mm amplifica presença. Mãos no quadril + peso deslocado dão direção clara sem exagero.

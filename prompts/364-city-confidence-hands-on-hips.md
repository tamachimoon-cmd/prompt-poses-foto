# 364 — City Confidence Hands on Hips

Finalidade: power pose urbana de confiança, ótima para retratos profissionais e lifestyle city.

## Prompt (copiar)

A confident woman standing with hands on her hips, elbows slightly bent, looking straight at the camera, against a cityscape backdrop, realistic photography style. Weight shifted to one leg, shoulders back but relaxed, full-body or three-quarter framing, golden hour light, 50mm lens, natural skin texture, direct eye contact, --ar 3:4

## Por que funciona

Mãos na cintura + contato visual direto projetam autoridade. Cotovelos levemente dobrados evitam pose rígida de "super-herói". Fundo urbano contextualiza sem distrair.

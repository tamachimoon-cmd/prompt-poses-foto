# 274 — Golden hour em contraluz com rebatedor dourado

Finalidade: retrato externo com rim dourado no cabelo e rosto preenchido de forma controlada.

Fórmula: sol baixo atrás + rebatedor como key + exposição pelo rosto + 85mm + textura + restrições

## Prompt (copiar)

Head-and-shoulders outdoor portrait of an adult woman in her mid-30s at golden hour, the low sun directly behind her and slightly to camera-right creating a warm rim of light in her hair and along her shoulders, face in open shade, a large gold-white reflector held in front of her slightly above head height and angled down to bounce sunlight back onto her face as the key, catchlights in the upper part of both eyes, shadows falling naturally below the nose, body turned 30 degrees to camera-left, weight on the back leg, one hand lightly holding the strap of a canvas bag at shoulder height, soft genuine smile, olive linen shirt, field of dry grass behind rendered as warm bokeh, exposure set for the face so the background glows without clipping the skin, 85mm lens at f/2.8, natural skin texture with light freckles and visible pores, warm but not orange skin tone, both hands complete with five natural fingers each, natural joint angles, realistic anatomy, no lens-flare wash over the face, no plastic skin.

## Por que funciona

Com o sol atrás, ele vira rim e o rosto fica em sombra aberta; o rebatedor passa a ser a luz principal controlável. Segundo Joe Edelman, rebater de cima (e não de baixo) mantém sombras abaixo do nariz e catchlights no alto da pupila. O dourado aquece, mas pode ficar laranja rápido; por isso pedir pele quente sem alaranjar.

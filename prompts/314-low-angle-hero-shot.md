# 314 — Low Angle Hero Shot

Finalidade: pose que transmite poder e presença, ângulo de baixo.

## Prompt (copiar)

Low-angle full-body shot of an adult model standing tall with hands in pockets, chin slightly down but eyes looking into lens, confident posture, camera near knee height looking up, dramatic rim lighting from behind, urban or open background, 28mm lens, heroic composition, realistic photography, strong presence.

## Por que funciona

Ângulo baixo alonga e empodera; rim light separa do fundo e adiciona drama controlado.

# 405 — Natural Walking Mid-Stride

Finalidade: pose de caminhada natural em movimento, capturando o momento da passada. Para street style ou lifestyle dinâmico.

## Prompt (copiar)

Create a full-body street-style photo of an adult person walking across a quiet pedestrian plaza in casual outfit. Capture the moment when the front foot has just touched the ground and the back heel is lifted. Let the arms swing gently in opposite directions to the legs. Keep the head upright and gaze ahead. Photograph from a slight side angle with the whole body and both shoes visible. Leave space in front of the subject. Soft daylight and a simple background, photorealistic, sharp focus, 35mm lens, --ar 3:4

## Por que funciona

Momento específico da passada (frente no chão, calcanhar levantado) + balanço oposto dos braços cria movimento crível sem blur forçado. Espaço à frente evita corte do corpo.

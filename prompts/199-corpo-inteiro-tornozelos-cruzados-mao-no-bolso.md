# 199 — Corpo inteiro com tornozelos cruzados e mão no bolso

Finalidade: pose em pé descontraída para lookbook ou e-commerce de moda masculina/feminina.

## Prompt (copiar)

Full-length lookbook photograph of an adult model in her early 30s standing on a light grey seamless, body angled about 30 degrees to camera-right, weight on the back leg, front foot crossed lightly over the back ankle with the toe touching the floor, knees soft and not locked, right hand in the trouser pocket with the thumb outside, left arm relaxed with the elbow slightly bent and a small gap between arm and torso, shoulders relaxed, chin level, soft smile toward camera, cream cable-knit sweater, straight-leg mid-blue jeans and white leather sneakers, large softbox key camera-left with a white bounce on camera-right, 70mm lens at f/5.6, camera at waist height, true-to-life fabric color, sharp eyes, both feet fully visible, both hands complete, no locked knees, no arms pinned to the sides.

## Por que funciona

Cruzar o pé da frente sobre o tornozelo cria linha e assimetria sem exagero. Mão no bolso com polegar para fora dá tarefa à mão; espaço entre braço e tronco evita braços colados. Câmera na altura da cintura não encurta as pernas.

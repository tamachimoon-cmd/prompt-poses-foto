# 347 — Hyperreal mechanic garage

Finalidade: retrato ambiental hiper-realista com imperfeições e sensor noise.

## Prompt (copiar)

A candid medium shot of a mechanic resting in a dimly lit garage, wiping grease from his forehead, directional harsh overhead fluorescent lighting, shot on a Hasselblad X1D, 50mm f/1.8 lens, Kodak Portra 400 color science, unretouched skin, visible pores, deep wrinkles, sweat beads, ISO 1600 sensor noise, slight chromatic aberration on the edges, raw unedited photography, hyper-detailed.

## Por que funciona

Câmera específica + ruído de sensor + aberração cromática + suor/poros são os sinais mais fortes de “foto real” para Flux e modelos modernos.

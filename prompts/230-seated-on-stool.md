# 230 — Seated on Stool

Finalidade: pose sentada de corpo inteiro com contato claro das mãos.

## Prompt (copiar)

Create a full-body studio portrait of an adult ceramic artist wearing a rust-colored work jacket and dark trousers seated near the front of a simple wooden stool. Both feet rest flat on the floor, one slightly ahead of the other. The torso leans forward a little from the hips. Rest each hand loosely on its corresponding thigh, with fingers relaxed and visible. Keep the head upright and gaze toward the camera. Include the entire stool and both shoes, with space below the feet. Soft side lighting and a neutral backdrop.

## Por que funciona

Pontos de contato (mãos nas coxas, pés no chão) estabilizam a pose. Espaço abaixo dos pés evita corte.

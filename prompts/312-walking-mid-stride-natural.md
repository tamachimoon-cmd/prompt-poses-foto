# 312 — Walking Mid-Stride

Finalidade: pose em movimento natural, captura energia de rua.

## Prompt (copiar)

Full-body street photograph of an adult model walking toward camera, front foot just touching ground, back heel lifted, arms swinging naturally opposite to legs, head upright looking slightly to the side, quiet urban plaza, soft daylight, 35mm lens, slight motion blur on feet, documentary style, realistic candid moment.

## Por que funciona

Momento específico da passada evita o "congelado artificial"; movimento dos braços equilibra a composição.

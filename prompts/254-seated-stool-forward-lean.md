# 254 — Seated stool forward lean

Finalidade: pose sentada natural em banco ou cubo com inclinação para frente.

## Prompt (copiar)

Photorealistic full-body or three-quarter studio portrait of an adult seated near the front of a simple wooden stool, both feet flat on the floor one slightly ahead, torso leaning forward a little from the hips, hands resting loosely on thighs with fingers relaxed and visible, soft side lighting, neutral backdrop, 50mm lens, natural expression.

## Por que funciona

Inclinação a partir do quadril + mãos com função clara evitam dedos deformados e pose rígida; pés no chão dão estabilidade realista.

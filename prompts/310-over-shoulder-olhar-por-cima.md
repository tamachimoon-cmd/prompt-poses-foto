# 310 — Over the Shoulder Look

Finalidade: pose misteriosa e envolvente, boa para fashion e lifestyle.

## Prompt (copiar)

Medium shot of an adult model turned three-quarters away from camera, looking back over the shoulder with a soft genuine smile, one hand lightly touching hair or collar, in soft window light indoor, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, realistic photography, natural catchlights in eyes.

## Por que funciona

O olhar por cima do ombro cria conexão e mistério simultâneos; o giro do corpo adiciona dinamismo.

Finalidade: retrato casual outdoor morning.

Casual lifestyle photo of a 26-year-old woman in a relaxed cotton top, seated at an outdoor cafe table with a coffee cup, morning open shade setting with warm bounce light from the table surface, 35mm equivalent at f/4, soft open shade illumination with no harsh shadows, unedited raw look, slight motion blur in background pedestrians, natural skin texture, imperfect candid composition.

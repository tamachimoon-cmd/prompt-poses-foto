Corporate headshot of a 34-year-old woman in a fitted navy blazer and white shirt, confident and relaxed expression, direct eye contact, natural skin texture with visible pores, subtle catchlight in both eyes, shoulders-up framing, clean mid-grey seamless backdrop, Canon EOS R5, 85mm at f/4, soft window light from camera left, ISO 200, moderate depth of field, slight shadow falloff on the right side of the face

# 252 — Contrapposto detalhado de estúdio

Finalidade: corpo inteiro com curva em S natural para editorial ou lookbook.

Fórmula: pose espacial completa + luz de octabox + lente de retrato.

## Prompt (copiar)

Full-length studio photograph of an adult model wearing a tailored charcoal blazer, white shirt and dark trousers, standing in relaxed contrapposto: weight fully on the right leg, left knee softly bent and angled inward, left hip elevated with slight lateral tilt, right shoulder dropped counter to the hip, shoulders mostly square to camera, right hand resting lightly on the hip bone, left arm hanging straight with slight elbow bend, head turned 15 degrees right, chin level, gaze direct and confident, clean light grey seamless cyclorama, large octabox key light camera-left at 45 degrees slightly above eye level, subtle rim light on the far shoulder, 85mm lens at f/2.8, sharp eyes, natural skin texture, visible fabric weave, professional editorial photography, no extra limbs, both hands complete and correctly formed.

## Por que funciona

Descrição espacial completa do contrapposto (peso, quadril, ombro, cabeça) dá ao modelo uma figura natural em S. Octabox a 45 graus esculpe o rosto sem achatar; rim separa do fundo.

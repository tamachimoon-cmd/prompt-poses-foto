# 229 — Relaxed Three-Quarter Portrait

Finalidade: retrato editorial com peso deslocado e torso virado.

## Prompt (copiar)

Create a realistic editorial portrait of an adult ceramic artist wearing a rust-colored work jacket and dark trousers. They stand with their weight on the back leg and their torso turned slightly away from the camera. Their head turns gently toward the lens, chin level and shoulders relaxed. Both arms hang naturally with loose hands visible beside the hips. Frame from mid-thigh upward at eye level. Plain warm gray background, soft window light from the left, natural expression and skin texture.

## Por que funciona

Descrição de peso, torso e mãos separadas reduz anatomia quebrada. Enquadramento claro evita crop acidental.

# 418 — Seated Cross-Legged Zen

Finalidade: pose calma e equilibrada para lifestyle, wellness ou editorial intimista.

## Prompt (copiar)

Photorealistic photograph of an adult model sitting cross-legged on a soft surface or floor, hands resting relaxed on the knees, upright posture, relaxed and content expression with soft smile, looking slightly off-camera or at camera, cozy indoor setting with soft window light from the left, natural skin texture, 50mm lens, eye-level, shallow depth of field, both hands and feet visible, photorealistic lifestyle photography.

## Por que funciona

Posição cruzada + mãos nos joelhos cria estabilidade e paz visual. Luz de janela adiciona naturalidade sem artificialidade de estúdio.

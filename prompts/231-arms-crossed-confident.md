# 231 — Arms crossed confident stance

Finalidade: pose de confiança com braços cruzados para retratos corporativos, executivos ou autoridade.

## Prompt (copiar)

Waist-up or three-quarter studio portrait of an adult model standing with arms crossed loosely in front of the chest, shoulders relaxed and slightly angled, confident direct gaze at camera, chin level, soft three-point lighting or large softbox with subtle rim, clean gray or white seamless backdrop, 85mm lens at f/2.8, camera at eye level, sharp focus on eyes, natural skin texture, professional attire, approachable yet strong presence, photorealistic commercial headshot style, no tension in the arms or shoulders.

## Por que funciona

Braços cruzados de forma solta comunicam confiança sem agressividade. Ombros relaxados evitam rigidez. Iluminação de três pontos dá volume profissional.

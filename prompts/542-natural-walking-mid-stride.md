# 542 — Natural Walking Mid-Stride

Finalidade: pose de caminhada natural de corpo inteiro para street style.

## Prompt (copiar)

Create a full-body street-style photo of an adult model walking across a quiet pedestrian plaza in casual outfit. Capture the moment when the front foot has just touched the ground and the back heel is lifted. Let the arms swing gently in opposite directions to the legs. Keep the head upright and gaze ahead. Photograph from a slight side angle with the whole body and both shoes visible. Leave space in front of the subject. Soft daylight and a simple background, photorealistic, 35mm lens feel.

## Por que funciona

Momento específico (pé da frente no chão, calcanhar traseiro levantado) + braços opostos criam movimento crível sem blur.

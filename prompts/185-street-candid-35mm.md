# 185 — Street Candid 35mm

Finalidade: fotografia de rua espontânea, documental.

## Prompt (copiar)

Candid street photography of an adult model walking or standing in an urban environment, natural posture mid-stride or relaxed, available light, 35mm lens, slight motion blur on background, documentary style, realistic skin and clothing texture, Kodak Portra or similar film grain, eye-level or slight low angle.

## Por que funciona

35mm captura contexto ambiental; candid e grain dão autenticidade de rua.

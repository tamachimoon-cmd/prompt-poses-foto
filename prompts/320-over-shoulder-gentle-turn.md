# 320 — Over Shoulder Gentle Turn

Finalidade: olhar de mistério controlado, três-quartos.

## Prompt (copiar)

Editorial portrait of an adult model. Body faces away at three-quarter angle, head turns gently back toward camera without twisting shoulders. Arms relaxed at sides. Frame from waist up with space in the direction of the gaze. Quiet garden or soft outdoor background, diffused daylight, calm expression, neck turn comfortable, face in three-quarter view, realistic photography.

## Por que funciona

Giro suave da cabeça sem forçar ombros cria conexão e profundidade sem distorção.

# 190 — Squinch e testa à frente no headshot

Finalidade: headshot de confiança com microexpressão de olhos e mandíbula definida.

## Prompt (copiar)

Tight headshot of an adult model in his mid-30s, chest turned about 30 degrees to camera-left, face turned back toward the lens, forehead pushed slightly forward and down toward the camera so the jawline reads clean and the skin under the chin is taut, back shoulder kept level and not dropped, subtle squinch: lower eyelids raised just slightly toward the pupils while the upper eyelids stay relaxed and open, expression between neutral and a small closed-mouth smile, navy crew-neck sweater, mid-grey seamless background, large softbox key camera-left slightly above eye level, white reflector below the chin, 105mm lens at f/5.6, both eyes sharp, natural skin texture, no wide-eyed stare, no full squint, no head tilt backward.

## Por que funciona

O squinch mexe só na pálpebra inferior: tira o olhar de "susto" sem virar careta. Testa para a frente e para baixo estica a pele sob o queixo e marca a mandíbula; não baixar o ombro de trás evita volume no pescoço. Descrever as duas pálpebras separadamente impede o modelo de fechar os olhos.

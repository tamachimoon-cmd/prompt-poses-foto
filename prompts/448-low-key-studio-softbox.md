Close-up low-key studio portrait of a 30-year-old woman, direct gaze, natural skin texture with visible pores and fine skin lines, dark neutral seamless background, Canon EOS R5, 85mm at f/4, single softbox key light 45 degrees camera left and above eye level, deep shadow falloff on the right side of the face, no bright fill light, controlled highlights, realistic skin texture, negative prompt: blown highlights, flat lighting, plastic skin, over-smoothed face, extra fingers, watermark, bright fill light.

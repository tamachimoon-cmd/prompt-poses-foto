# 311 — Seated on Stool — Lean Forward

Finalidade: pose sentada engajada, ideal para retratos mais íntimos.

## Prompt (copiar)

Full-body shot of an adult model seated on the edge of a simple wooden stool, torso leaning slightly forward from the hips, both hands resting loosely on thighs, feet flat on floor one slightly ahead, upright head with direct gaze, soft side lighting, neutral studio backdrop, 50mm lens, realistic proportions, no awkward limb angles.

## Por que funciona

O lean forward cria proximidade e interesse; mãos visíveis e pés plantados evitam poses truncadas.

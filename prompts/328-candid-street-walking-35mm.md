# 328 — Candid street walking 35mm

Finalidade: fotografia de rua candid de pessoa andando para reportagem ou lifestyle urbano.

## Prompt (copiar)

Candid street photograph of an adult walking naturally mid-stride through a city street, looking slightly to the side, natural posture, slight motion on feet, neon or ambient light reflections on wet pavement if applicable, shot on Leica or 35mm lens at f/2, documentary style, realistic skin texture, Kodak Portra or available light, photorealistic, no posed look.

## Por que funciona

Mid-stride + 35mm + available light cria movimento e autenticidade. Evitar “posed” reduz o look artificial.

# 297 — Seated Edge Bench Candid

Finalidade: pose sentada candid na borda de um banco, para lifestyle ou editorial relaxado.

## Prompt (copiar)

Medium shot of an adult model seated on the edge of a wooden bench, spine long, weight forward on the right hip, right forearm resting on the right thigh, left hand loose between the knees, head turned 20 degrees camera-right, gaze down and slightly away, jaw relaxed, natural window light from the side, soft shadows, shallow depth of field, realistic photography, lifestyle editorial.

## Por que funciona

Peso à frente e olhar desviado criam naturalidade; posição das mãos e braços evita poses rígidas de catálogo.

# 385 — Hands in Pockets Casual

Finalidade: pose casual e relaxada com mãos nos bolsos para lifestyle urbano ou street style.

## Prompt (copiar)

Photorealistic full-body photograph of an adult man standing with both hands in front pockets, thumbs slightly visible, weight shifted to one leg, shoulders relaxed and slightly angled, casual confident expression looking toward the camera, against an urban wall or street backdrop with mild bokeh, realistic photography style, soft natural daylight, 50mm lens at f/2.8, eye-level camera, true-to-life clothing folds and skin texture, both feet visible.

## Por que funciona

Mãos nos bolsos reduzem tensão e dão ar descontraído. Deslocamento de peso evita postura rígida. Polegares visíveis evitam mãos completamente escondidas e problemáticas.

# 209 — iPhone Candid Real Photo

Finalidade: look de foto de celular real, sem estúdio.

## Prompt (copiar)

A real photo a friend took on an iPhone of a woman in her late 20s with blonde hair pulled back, standing on a Paris street corner on a grey autumn day, weight on one leg, one gloved hand resting on a black metal bollard, looking away down the street. Natural skin with visible texture, crisp and sharp everywhere with no background blur, punchy HDR, slightly over-sharpened details, true-to-life colours. Not a fashion editorial, no studio light, no bokeh.

## Por que funciona

Explicitamente "iPhone" + "no bokeh" + candid framing combate o look de render. Modelos entendem o visual de smartphone.

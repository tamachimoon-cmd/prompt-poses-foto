# 249 — Mãos de relojoeiro com lupa, documental

Finalidade: close documental de ofício manual, foco em mãos e concentração.

## Prompt (copiar)

Intimate documentary portrait of an adult watchmaker in his seventies examining a small mechanical escapement through a brass loupe at his workbench, framing tight on face and hands, deep dignified character lines on weathered hands, natural skin pores and tactile micro-creases, focused downward gaze, soft north-facing workshop window light from camera-left creating a gentle triangle of light on the shadow-side cheek, dust motes visible in the light, tiny tools and screws on a green bench mat in soft focus, 85mm lens at f/1.8, subtle creamy background bokeh, Kodak Portra 400 tone with fine grain, 4:5 aspect ratio, no plastic skin, no over-smoothing, no extra fingers, no text.

## Por que funciona

A ação minuciosa (lupa e mecanismo) concentra o olhar nas mãos e dá narrativa sem pose. Textura de pele em micro-dobras e luz lateral suave de janela revelam idade com dignidade. A referência a Portra 400 puxa tons de pele naturais e grão orgânico, como a fonte sugere.

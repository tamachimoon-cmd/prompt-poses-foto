# 369 — Candid street photo at a crosswalk

Finalidade: momento de rua não posado com movimento e framing off-center.

## Prompt (copiar)

Candid street photograph of a man in a rain jacket waiting at a crosswalk on a gray afternoon, glancing at his phone, a city bus passing behind him as a slight motion blur, wet asphalt reflections, 35mm lens at eye level, slightly off-center framing, muted colors, fine grain

## Por que funciona

Framing off-center + motion blur + 35mm documentário fazem parecer foto pega no ato, não staged. Reflexos molhados ajudam na física da luz.

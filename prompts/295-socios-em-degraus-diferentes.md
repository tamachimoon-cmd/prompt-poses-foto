# 295 — Sócios em degraus diferentes

Finalidade: retrato de dupla de sócios ou colegas com camaradagem e alturas equilibradas.

Fórmula: dois degraus + um apoiado no corrimão + outro inclinado para o parceiro + 50mm f/5.6 + luz suave + restrições

## Prompt (copiar)

Outdoor business portrait of two adult male business partners in their 40s on the wide concrete stairs outside a modern office, the first seated on a higher step leaning slightly on the metal handrail with his forearm resting over his raised knee, the second seated two steps lower and leaning back and toward his partner to unify the pair, both with upright posture, knees slightly higher than hips, hands resting loosely over the knees, both turned in toward each other at about 30 degrees with faces to the lens and relaxed genuine smiles, smart-casual blazers over knitwear, soft overcast light with a white bounce at camera position, 50mm lens at f/5.6 so both faces are sharp, natural skin texture on both faces, both hands complete with five natural fingers each, natural joint angles, realistic anatomy for both people, no merged limbs, no duplicated faces, no plastic skin.

## Por que funciona

Degraus diferentes equilibram a diferença de altura entre as duas pessoas, e a inclinação de um em direção ao outro cria a camaradagem, como no exemplo da fonte. Postura ereta com mãos sobre os joelhos evita que a pose sentada pareça casual demais.

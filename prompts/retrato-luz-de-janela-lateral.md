# Retrato com luz natural de janela

## Uso

Retrato suave e natural com luz de janela lateral, foco no olho mais próximo e textura real de pele.

## Prompt

```text
realistic portrait of [subject], soft window light from camera-left at 45 degrees, gentle falloff on right cheek, mid-tone skin with natural pore detail, neutral white balance, 85mm f/1.8, shallow depth of field, sharp focus on near eye, calm expression
```

## Fonte

- AI Tools Guidebook — Realistic Portrait Lighting Prompts
- https://aitoolsguidebook.com/en/articles/realistic-portrait-lighting-prompts/

## Dicas

Mantenha a direção da luz explícita (camera-left, 45°). Para fundo mais limpo, acrescente a cor/ambiente da parede.

## Prompt (copiar)

Create a full-body studio portrait of an adult seated near the front of a simple wooden stool. Both feet flat on the floor, one slightly ahead. Torso leans forward a little from the hips. Each hand rests loosely on its corresponding thigh, fingers relaxed. Head upright, gaze toward camera. Soft side lighting, neutral backdrop, 50mm, photorealistic --ar 3:4

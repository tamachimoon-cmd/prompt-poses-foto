# 226 — Zen cross-legged seated

Finalidade: pose sentada calma e equilibrada para shots de lifestyle, wellness ou indoor aconchegante.

## Prompt (copiar)

A woman sitting cross-legged on a soft surface, hands resting loosely on her knees with palms up, shoulders relaxed, slight smile, gaze soft toward camera, in a cozy indoor space with soft window light from the side, full body including feet, realistic photography style, natural fabric folds, photorealistic.

## Por que funciona

Posição de lótus ou cross-legged transmite paz. Mãos nas coxas evitam problemas de anatomia e criam equilíbrio visual. Ideal para mood sereno.

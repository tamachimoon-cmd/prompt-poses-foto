# 401 — Freckled window light portrait

Finalidade: close natural com sardas e luz de janela, estilo Portra.

## Prompt (copiar)

Close-up portrait of a freckled red-haired woman in a mustard sweater, looking slightly past the camera, soft window light from the right, shot on 85mm f/1.4, shallow depth of field, Kodak Portra 400, natural skin texture with visible freckles and pores.

## Por que funciona

Olhar levemente desviado + sardas explícitas + Portra matam o "olhando para a câmera sorrindo" de stock. Luz direcional da janela dá modelagem real.

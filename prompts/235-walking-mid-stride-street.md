# 235 — Walking mid-stride street

Finalidade: pose de caminhada natural em rua para street style ou lifestyle dinâmico.

## Prompt (copiar)

Full-body street-style photograph of an adult walking across a quiet plaza, front foot just touching ground, back heel lifted, arms swinging naturally opposite to legs, head upright, gaze ahead, slight side angle, soft daylight, both shoes visible, photorealistic 50mm —ar 3:4

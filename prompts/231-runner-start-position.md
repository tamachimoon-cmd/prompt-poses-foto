# 231 — Runner at the start

Finalidade: pose atlética de largada para sports portraits e dinamismo.

## Prompt (copiar)

Create a full-body sports portrait of an adult runner preparing to move on an empty track. One foot is forward with the knee bent, the other leg extends behind, and the torso leans slightly forward. Bend both elbows naturally, with the opposite arm ahead of the forward leg. Show the entire silhouette from a three-quarter side angle. Keep the hands relaxed rather than clenched. Early morning light, sharp subject, open space ahead, no motion blur, photorealistic.

## Por que funciona

Posição de largada é clara e anatômica. Braços opostos e inclinação dão tensão pronta para ação sem ser blur.

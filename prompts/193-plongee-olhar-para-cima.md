# 193 — Plongée suave com olhar para a lente

Finalidade: retrato de câmera alta para editorial intimista ou lifestyle, transmitindo leveza e proximidade.

## Prompt (copiar)

High-angle editorial portrait of an adult model in her early 30s seated on a light wooden studio floor, legs folded to one side, torso upright, hands resting loosely on the knee, camera positioned about one meter above her eye line and angled down at roughly 35 degrees, face lifted toward the lens with a calm, open expression, oversized sage-green knit sweater and wide-leg cream trousers, floor texture filling the frame as a clean backdrop, large overhead softbox slightly in front of her creating catchlights at the top of the eyes, 50mm lens at f/2.8, sharp eyes, natural skin texture, soft muted palette, no extreme bird's-eye distortion, no foreshortened legs, tasteful and fully clothed.

## Por que funciona

Câmera acima e apontada para baixo muda a leitura de poder: a pessoa parece mais leve e próxima. Dar ângulo concreto (1 m acima, 35°) evita o top shot total. O chão vira fundo limpo e a luz alta acompanha o ângulo, colocando catchlights no alto dos olhos.

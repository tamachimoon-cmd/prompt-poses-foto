# 291 — Inclinado para trás com mãos abertas na grade

Finalidade: retrato masculino poderoso, mas relaxado, em ambiente urbano.

Fórmula: pés na largura dos ombros + mãos abertas apoiadas atrás na grade + tronco inclinado + 50mm + luz lateral + restrições

## Prompt (copiar)

Three-quarter-length outdoor portrait of an adult man in his mid-40s leaning back against a waist-high steel bridge railing, feet shoulder-width apart, both arms spread out to the sides with the hands resting on the rail behind him, fingers wrapped naturally around the bar, torso leaning back slightly, shoulders open and relaxed, chin level, calm confident gaze into the lens, grey wool overcoat open over a black knit, river and city blurred behind, soft late-afternoon side light from camera-right with gentle fill from the sky, 50mm lens at f/2.8, natural skin texture with stubble and fine lines, both hands complete with five natural fingers each, natural joint angles, realistic anatomy, no strained elbows, no plastic skin.

## Por que funciona

A fonte descreve essa pose como poderosa e relaxada ao mesmo tempo: pés na largura dos ombros e mãos abertas num apoio atrás. A grade sustenta o peso, então os ombros relaxam e o peito abre sem rigidez.

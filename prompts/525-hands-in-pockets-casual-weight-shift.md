# 525 — Hands in Pockets Casual Weight Shift

Finalidade: pose casual e confiante para street style ou lifestyle urbano.

## Prompt (copiar)

A man standing with hands in his pockets, weight on one leg, relaxed shoulders, slight head tilt, exuding a relaxed and confident vibe, against a cityscape backdrop, realistic photography style, full-body shot, eye-level, 35mm lens, natural daylight, photorealistic --ar 3:4

## Por que funciona

Mãos nos bolsos + deslocamento de peso criam vibe descontraída sem parecer forçada. Fundo urbano completa o contexto.

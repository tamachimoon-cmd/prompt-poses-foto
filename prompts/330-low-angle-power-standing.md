# 330 — Low angle power standing

Finalidade: pose de poder em ângulo baixo para transmitir confiança ou autoridade.

## Prompt (copiar)

Full-body low-angle photograph of an adult standing confidently, weight on both feet or slight shift, shoulders back, arms relaxed or one hand in pocket, looking down toward camera with strong gaze, shot from knee height with 24mm wide lens, dramatic perspective, soft studio or natural light, natural skin and clothing texture, photorealistic, both feet visible, no distortion on hands.

## Por que funciona

Ângulo baixo + lente wide amplifica presença. Descrever peso e ombros evita postura rígida.

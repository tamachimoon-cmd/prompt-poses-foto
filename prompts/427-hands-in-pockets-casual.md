# 427 — Hands in Pockets Casual

Finalidade: pose casual de rua ou lifestyle com mãos parcialmente visíveis (polegares), reduzindo risco de dedos deformados.

## Prompt (copiar)

Create a casual portrait of an adult model in a jacket and straight-leg trousers. The subject stands with feet comfortably apart, weight resting on one leg, and both hands partly tucked into the front trouser pockets with thumbs visible. Keep the elbows close to the body and shoulders lowered. Use a three-quarter composition that includes the knees, photographed at chest height. Quiet outdoor wall, soft afternoon light, relaxed expression, photorealistic photography.

## Por que funciona

Polegares visíveis dão âncora anatômica às mãos. Peso em uma perna cria silhueta natural.

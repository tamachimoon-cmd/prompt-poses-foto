Finalidade: retrato externo com contraluz quente de golden hour.

## Prompt (copiar)

Portrait of a man in his early 30s, short beard, denim jacket, standing on a city street at golden hour, warm backlight, natural skin, shot on 35mm, candid and unposed, rim light on hair, shallow depth of field.

Backlight de golden hour cria rim light natural, 35mm mantém perspectiva ambiental, pose não posada reduz artificialidade, pele natural evita plastificação.

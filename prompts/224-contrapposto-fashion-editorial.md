# 224 — Contrapposto fashion editorial

Finalidade: pose de moda em pé com contrapposto clássico para lookbook ou editorial de alta costura.

## Prompt (copiar)

Full-length fashion editorial photograph of an adult model standing in relaxed contrapposto, weight shifted to the back leg, opposite knee softly bent, one hand lightly on the hip with fingers relaxed, other arm hanging with natural gap from torso, shoulders dropped and slightly angled, chin level, direct soft gaze at camera, wearing tailored contemporary clothing, large softbox key light from camera-left with subtle fill, light gray seamless backdrop, 70mm lens at f/5.6, camera at waist height, true-to-life skin and fabric texture, both feet fully visible, natural hands, photorealistic Vogue-style editorial.

## Por que funciona

Contrapposto cria linha e movimento sutil. Mão no quadril dá função sem tensão. Softbox controlada evita sombras duras e mantém o foco no tecido e na pose.

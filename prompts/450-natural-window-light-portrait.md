# 450 — Natural Window-Light Portrait

Finalidade: retrato natural com luz de janela e textura realista de pele.

## Prompt (copiar)

Candid portrait of a woman in her late 20s with light freckles and natural skin texture, relaxed half-smile, cream knit sweater, sunlit café window seat, soft natural window light, shot on 85mm, shallow depth of field, visible pores, no heavy retouching.

## Por que funciona

Luz de janela específica evita look artificial de studio, lente 85mm comprime e cria bokeh, textura de pele e sardinhas quebram o smoothing de IA, expressão relaxada soa humana.

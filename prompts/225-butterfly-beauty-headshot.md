# 225 — Butterfly beauty headshot

Finalidade: retrato beauty de estúdio com iluminação butterfly (paramount) para pele limpa e look comercial/editorial.

## Prompt (copiar)

Photorealistic beauty headshot of an adult model, medium close-up, butterfly lighting with softbox directly above and slightly in front creating a soft shadow under the nose and chin, subtle fill from below, pure white or light gray seamless backdrop, 85mm lens at f/2.0, eye-level camera, sharp focus on eyes with distinct catchlights, natural skin texture with visible pores, soft natural makeup, gentle smile, shoulders slightly angled, commercial beauty photography style, no plastic smoothing.

## Por que funciona

Butterfly lighting é a clássica de beauty: limpa, lisonjeira e previsível. 85mm + f/2 isola o rosto sem distorção. Textura explícita mata o look de boneca da IA.

# 359 — Hands in Pockets Weight Shift

Finalidade: pose casual de corpo inteiro ou três-quartos, relaxed e urbana.

## Prompt (copiar)

Full-body photograph of an adult model in a casual jacket and straight-leg trousers. The subject stands with feet comfortably apart, weight resting on one leg, and both hands partly tucked into the front trouser pockets with thumbs visible. Keep the elbows close to the body and shoulders lowered. Three-quarter composition including the knees, photographed at chest height. Quiet outdoor wall, soft afternoon light, relaxed expression, 50mm lens, natural skin texture, realistic photography style.

## Por que funciona

Polegares visíveis e cotovelos próximos controlam a postura dos braços. Peso em uma perna quebra a simetria rígida. Ideal para lifestyle sem parecer posado demais.

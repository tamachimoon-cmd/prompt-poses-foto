# 226 — Golden hour outdoor portrait

Finalidade: retrato outdoor com luz dourada para mood quente e editorial.

## Prompt (copiar)

Outdoor portrait of an adult model at golden hour, backlit by low sun creating a warm rim light around the hair, face gently lifted by soft reflected light from below. Shallow depth of field, warm bokeh from distant foliage. 135mm lens at f/2, compressed background. Golden tones, slight haze, photorealistic, natural skin texture, relaxed expression, weight shifted on one leg, hands relaxed, no lens flare artifacts.

## Por que funciona

Rim light dourado cria separação e calor. 135mm comprime o fundo e lisonjeia. Pose com deslocamento de peso evita rigidez.

# 358 — Relaxed Three-Quarter Portrait

Finalidade: retrato editorial de meio corpo com peso deslocado e torso ligeiramente virado, ideal para lifestyle e perfil profissional.

## Prompt (copiar)

Create a realistic editorial portrait of an adult model wearing a rust-colored work jacket and dark trousers. They stand with their weight on the back leg and their torso turned slightly away from the camera. Their head turns gently toward the lens, chin level and shoulders relaxed. Both arms hang naturally with loose hands visible beside the hips. Frame from mid-thigh upward at eye level. Plain warm gray background, soft window light from the left, natural expression and skin texture, 85mm lens at f/2.8, shallow depth of field.

## Por que funciona

Descrição de peso, torso e cabeça separados evita pose rígida. Mãos soltas e visíveis reduzem erros de anatomia. Luz de janela + fundo neutro dá resultado profissional sem parecer studio forçado.

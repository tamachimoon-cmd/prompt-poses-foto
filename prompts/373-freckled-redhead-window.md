# 373 — Close-up freckled red-haired woman

Finalidade: close natural com sardas e luz de janela para textura de pele real.

## Prompt (copiar)

Close-up portrait of a freckled red-haired woman in a mustard sweater, looking slightly past the camera, soft window light from the right, shot on 85mm f/1.4, shallow depth of field, Kodak Portra 400 --ar 4:5 --style raw

## Por que funciona

Sardas + olhar past camera + luz de janela nomeada + Portra. Evita simetria perfeita e skin plastic.

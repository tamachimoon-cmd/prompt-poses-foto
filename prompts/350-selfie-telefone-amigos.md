# 350 — Selfie de telefone com amigos

Finalidade: look de snapshot casual de smartphone.

## Prompt (copiar)

Casual smartphone photo of four adult friends laughing around a small kitchen table after dinner, half-empty glasses, candle and plates, warm ceiling light with slightly blown highlights, wide phone lens with edge distortion, one person partly cut off at frame edge, slight noise, unposed, natural expressions.

## Por que funciona

Lente de telefone + framing imperfeito + highlights estourados + corte de pessoa são assinaturas de foto real de celular.

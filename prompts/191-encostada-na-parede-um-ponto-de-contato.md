# 191 — Encostada na parede com um único ponto de contato

Finalidade: pose apoiada relaxada para editorial urbano ou lifestyle, meio corpo a corpo inteiro.

## Prompt (copiar)

Three-quarter-length photograph of an adult model in her early 30s leaning against a pale concrete wall with real weight on a single point of contact, only the right shoulder blade touching the wall, spine long rather than curving into the wall, torso angled about 45 degrees away from the wall toward camera, weight settled on the back leg, front knee soft, left hand pressed lightly flat on the wall at hip height with the elbow softly bent, right hand resting in the coat pocket with the thumb outside, small visible gap between upper arms and torso, camel wool coat over a white shirt and dark straight trousers, gaze slightly off-camera, soft overcast daylight, 70mm lens at f/2.8, sharp eyes, natural skin texture, editorial street photography, no flat square contact with the wall, no hovering lean, both hands complete.

## Por que funciona

Um único ponto de contato com peso real gera diagonal; costas inteiras coladas na parede ficam chapadas e "apoiadas". A segunda mão na parede dá função ao braço e estabiliza a pose sem dobrar o peso. Pedir espaço entre braço e tronco evita braço achatado.

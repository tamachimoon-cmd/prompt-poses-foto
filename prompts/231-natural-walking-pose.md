# 231 — Natural Walking Pose

Finalidade: pose de caminhada natural em rua, momento específico da passada.

## Prompt (copiar)

Create a full-body street-style photo of an adult subject walking across a quiet pedestrian plaza. Capture the moment when the front foot has just touched the ground and the back heel is lifted. Let the arms swing gently in opposite directions to the legs. Keep the head upright and gaze ahead. Photograph from a slight side angle with the whole body and both shoes visible. Leave space in front of the subject. Soft daylight and a simple background.

## Por que funciona

Capturar um instante específico da passada evita movimento ambíguo. Braços opostos às pernas dão naturalidade.

# 196 — Retrato ambiental com regra dos terços e linhas guia

Finalidade: retrato de profissional no seu ambiente de trabalho, com contexto e leitura clara.

## Prompt (copiar)

Environmental portrait of an adult woodworker in his early 40s standing at the end of a long workbench in his workshop, composed by the rule of thirds with his eyes on the upper-right third intersection and the remaining two thirds of the frame showing the workshop, the edges of the workbench and a row of hanging tools forming leading lines that converge toward him, hip resting lightly against the bench, arms loosely crossed with one elbow lower than the other, relaxed confident expression, canvas apron over a denim shirt, warm window light from camera-right mixed with soft ambient fill, 35mm lens at f/4 keeping the environment legible but slightly softer than the subject, sharp eyes, natural skin and wood textures, documentary-editorial look, no clutter in front of the face, no lines leading out of the frame.

## Por que funciona

Olhos no terço superior deixam espaço para o contexto contar a história. Linhas guia (bancada, ferramentas) que convergem para a pessoa conduzem o olhar; a fonte alerta para linhas que apontam para fora do quadro. 35 mm em f/4 mantém o ambiente legível sem competir com o rosto.

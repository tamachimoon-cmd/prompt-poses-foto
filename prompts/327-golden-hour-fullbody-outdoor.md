# 327 — Golden hour full body outdoor

Finalidade: retrato ambiental de corpo inteiro em golden hour para lifestyle ou moda outdoor.

## Prompt (copiar)

Full-body photorealistic portrait of an adult standing in a natural outdoor field at golden hour, weight shifted to one leg in relaxed stance, arms relaxed at sides with natural gap, soft backlight creating rim light on hair and shoulders, warm amber tones, natural skin texture, shot on 50mm lens at f/2.8, camera at waist height, shallow depth of field on background, Kodak Portra 400 film look, both feet visible, photorealistic.

## Por que funciona

Golden hour + rim light + peso deslocado dão profundidade e naturalidade. Especificar lente e filme evita o look digital flat.

# 186 — Low Angle Power Pose

Finalidade: pose de autoridade com ângulo baixo.

## Prompt (copiar)

Low-angle full-body shot of an adult model standing with feet apart, hands on hips or arms crossed, looking down at the camera, dramatic sky or urban background, strong rim or side light, 24mm lens for perspective, heroic composition, sharp focus, natural proportions, no distortion on face.

## Por que funciona

Ângulo baixo amplifica poder; pose aberta reforça a dominância visual.

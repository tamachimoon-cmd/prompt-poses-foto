# 229 — Natural walking mid-stride

Finalidade: captura de caminhada natural em rua ou praça para lifestyle e street photography.

## Prompt (copiar)

Create a full-body street-style photo of an adult person walking across a quiet pedestrian plaza in casual outfit. Capture the moment when the front foot has just touched the ground and the back heel is lifted. Let the arms swing gently in opposite directions to the legs. Keep the head upright and gaze ahead. Photograph from a slight side angle with the whole body and both shoes visible. Leave space in front of the subject. Soft daylight and a simple background, photorealistic, natural motion freeze.

## Por que funciona

Descrever o momento exato da passada (frente no chão, calcanhar levantado) evita poses rígidas. Braços opostos dão ritmo natural.

# 520 — Over Shoulder Coy Look

Finalidade: pose misteriosa e envolvente para lifestyle ou fashion outdoor.

## Prompt (copiar)

A woman turned slightly away from the camera, looking back over her shoulder with a gentle smile, in a sunlit park with soft bokeh, realistic photography style, three-quarter shot, eye-level, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, photorealistic --ar 3:4

## Por que funciona

Olhar por cima do ombro cria conexão e mistério simultaneamente. Luz solar suave favorece pele natural.

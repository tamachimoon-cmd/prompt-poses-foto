# 214 — Golden Hour Street Candid

Candid street portrait of a man in his early 30s with short beard, denim jacket, standing on a city sidewalk at golden hour, warm backlight creating rim light on hair and shoulders, natural skin, unposed three-quarter stance, shot on 35mm f/1.8, shallow depth of field, warm amber tones, subtle lens flare, Kodak Portra 400 film grain, documentary style.

Backlight + candid + 35mm cria profundidade e realismo de rua. Evita o "posed studio" que a maioria dos modelos entrega por padrão.

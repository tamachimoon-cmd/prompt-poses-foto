# 229 — Phone selfie beach friends

Finalidade: snapshot de celular, candid e imperfeito, estilo social media autêntico.

## Prompt (copiar)

Phone photo of two adult friends taking a selfie on a crowded beach boardwalk, slightly tilted framing, harsh midday sun, squinting eyes, wind in their hair, a little lens flare, casual and unposed, natural skin texture with pores, handheld smartphone look, photorealistic.

## Por que funciona

Tilted framing + midday harsh + squinting + flare + wind = imperfeições reais de telefone. Evita o look de estúdio perfeito.

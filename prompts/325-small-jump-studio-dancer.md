# 325 — Small Jump Studio Dancer

Finalidade: energia e movimento estático controlado.

## Prompt (copiar)

Full-body action photo of an adult dancer making a small vertical jump in a bright studio. Both feet just above the floor, knees slightly bent, arms open to the sides below shoulder height. Torso upright, expression relaxed. Fast-shutter look, clear hands and feet, soft shadow directly below. Generous space above head and below shoes. Plain background, even light, realistic photography.

## Por que funciona

Salto pequeno e momento congelado reduz artefatos de movimento; espaço generoso evita cortes.

# 05 — Retrato ambiental de oficina

## Prompt
Environmental portrait, 28mm lens, f/4, adult craftsperson at a workbench, hands on a tool, elbows bent naturally, face turned to camera, stance grounded. Mixed light: warm tungsten practicals behind, cool daylight from a side window, real shadows on tools and wood. Moderate depth of field, documentary full-frame photo, fine grain, warm grade inspired by Kodak Gold.

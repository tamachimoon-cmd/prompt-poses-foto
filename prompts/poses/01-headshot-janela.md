# 01 — Headshot com luz de janela

Uso: retrato profissional natural. Sujeito adulto. Troque roupa e fundo.

## Prompt
Tight head-and-shoulders portrait of an adult, 85mm lens, f/1.8, shoulders turned about 30 degrees, chin slightly down, relaxed closed-mouth smile, eyes to camera. Soft north window key at 45 degrees, white reflector fill, gentle cheekbone shadow, accurate catchlights. Neutral wall, smooth bokeh. Visible pores, fine hair, no plastic skin. Full-frame photo, subtle grain, warm neutral film grade similar to Portra 400.

## Negativo
plastic skin, waxy face, extra fingers, distorted eyes, heavy beauty filter, text, watermark, oversaturated reds

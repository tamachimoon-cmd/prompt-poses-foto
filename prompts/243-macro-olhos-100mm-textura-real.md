# 243 — Macro de olhos em 100mm com textura real

Finalidade: beauty/detalhe de olhar para campanha de óculos, cosméticos neutros ou editorial de close extremo.

## Prompt (copiar)

Extreme close-up beauty photograph of an adult model in her early 30s, framing from mid-forehead to just below the nose, eyes looking slightly above the lens, detailed iris texture with visible color patterns, individual eyelashes clearly defined, natural brows with fine hairs, skin pores and fine texture visible around the eyes, a few faint freckles, minimal neutral makeup, single large soft light from camera-left slightly above creating one clear catchlight in each eye, white reflector below, 100mm macro lens at f/2.8 with focus on the near eye, rest of the face falling gently out of focus, neutral color grade, fine film grain, professional beauty photography, 4:5 aspect ratio, no heavy retouching, no plastic or porcelain skin, no doll eyes, no mismatched catchlights.

## Por que funciona

A macro 100 mm comprime e mostra íris, cílios e poros, exatamente os detalhes que o modelo costuma "alisar". Uma única fonte de luz garante catchlights coerentes nos dois olhos. Proibir pele de porcelana e olhos de boneca reforça o realismo no ponto mais sensível do rosto.

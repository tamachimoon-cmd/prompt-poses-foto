# 510 — Corporate headshot profissional

Finalidade: LinkedIn ou perfil corporativo limpo.

Fórmula: shoulders-up + luz softbox + fundo seamless + lente 85mm + pele real.

## Prompt (copiar)

Corporate headshot of a 34-year-old woman in a fitted navy blazer and white shirt, confident and relaxed expression, direct eye contact, natural skin texture with visible pores, subtle catchlight in both eyes, shoulders-up framing, clean mid-grey seamless backdrop, 85mm at f/4, soft window light from camera left or softbox equivalent, moderate depth of field, slight shadow falloff on the right side of the face.

## Por que funciona

Catchlights nos dois olhos e poros visíveis evitam o look de manequim. Fundo cinza neutro é atemporal.

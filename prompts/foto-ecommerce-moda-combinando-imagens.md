# Foto de moda e-commerce combinando imagens

## Uso

Compor uma foto de e-commerce de corpo inteiro: roupa de uma imagem vestida pela pessoa de outra, com luz e sombras ajustadas ao ambiente.

## Prompt

```text
Create a professional e-commerce fashion photo. Take the blue floral dress from the first image and let the woman from the second image wear it. Generate a realistic, full-body shot of the woman wearing the dress, with the lighting and shadows adjusted to match the outdoor environment.
```

## Fonte

- Google AI for Developers — Gemini API: Nano Banana image generation (Prompting guide and strategies)
- https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/image-generation

## Dicas

Template da fonte: "Create a new image by combining the elements from the provided images. Take the [element from image 1] and place it with/on the [element from image 2]. The final image should be a [description of the final scene]."

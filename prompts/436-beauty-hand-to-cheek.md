# 436 — Beauty Hand to Cheek

Finalidade: close de beleza com mão na bochecha e butterfly lighting.

## Prompt (copiar)

Beauty portrait hand-to-cheek, subtle smile, butterfly lighting, studio high-key, 105mm lens, elegant crop, adult model with natural skin texture, photorealistic --ar 4:5

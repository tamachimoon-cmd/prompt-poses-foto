Finalidade: natural e intimista em interior.

## Prompt (copiar)

Lifestyle photograph of an adult model seated on a window sill, soft natural window light from camera-left creating gentle side illumination and soft shadow falloff on the right side of the face, wearing a cream knit sweater, relaxed posture with one knee up and arms resting naturally, looking slightly out the window then back toward camera, warm interior tones, 35mm lens at f/2.0, shallow depth of field, authentic candid feel, natural skin texture, photorealistic.

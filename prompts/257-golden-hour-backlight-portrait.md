# 257 — Golden hour backlight portrait

Finalidade: retrato outdoor com luz dourada e rim light.

## Prompt (copiar)

Photorealistic portrait of an adult at golden hour, warm sunlight backlighting the hair into soft rim glow, standing in open field or street, gentle smile, 50mm or 85mm lens, creamy bokeh, Kodak Portra 400 tones, subtle film grain, natural skin with visible texture, slight breeze in hair.

## Por que funciona

Backlight + rim glow específico evita saturação exagerada de "sunset"; Portra força paleta fotográfica real.

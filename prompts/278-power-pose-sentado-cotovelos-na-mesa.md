# 278 — Power pose sentado com cotovelos na mesa

Finalidade: retrato executivo engajado, inclinado para a câmera.

Fórmula: terço da frente da cadeira + inclinação a partir do quadril + antebraços na mesa + 85mm + restrições

## Prompt (copiar)

Chest-up corporate portrait of an adult woman in her mid-40s seated at a clean walnut desk, sitting on the front third of the chair with the spine long, leaning slightly forward from the hips, both forearms resting on the desk with elbows soft, hands loosely clasped with fingers interlaced naturally, shoulders down and relaxed away from the ears, chin slightly forward and down, direct engaged gaze into the lens, subtle confident smile, tailored black blazer over an ivory blouse, softly blurred office window line behind, large softbox key camera-left at 45 degrees with white bounce camera-right, 85mm lens at f/2.8, sharp eyes, natural skin texture with visible pores and fine lines, both hands complete with five natural fingers each, natural joint angles, realistic anatomy, interlaced fingers clearly separated, no slouching, no collapsed chest, no plastic skin.

## Por que funciona

A inclinação a partir do quadril aproxima o rosto da lente e transmite atenção sem curvar as costas. Sentar no terço da frente mantém a coluna longa. Antebraços apoiados ancoram o quadro e dão função às mãos.

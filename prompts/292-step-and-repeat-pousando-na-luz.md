# 292 — Step and repeat pousando na luz

Finalidade: foto de moda com movimento natural, capturada no ponto exato da luz.

Fórmula: dois ou três passos repetidos + parar na marca de luz + roupa em movimento + 85mm + softbox lateral + restrições

## Prompt (copiar)

Full-length studio fashion photograph of an adult woman in her late 20s caught at the moment she lands after two walking steps toward camera, front foot just planted on a marked spot in the best light, back heel lifting, weight transferring forward, arms swinging naturally with the elbows softly bent, the hem of her wide-leg camel trousers and long cream trench coat still moving, torso upright, head level and eyes on the lens, relaxed mouth, light grey seamless, one large strip softbox camera-left at 45 degrees plus a fill panel camera-right so she is evenly lit exactly at the landing spot, 85mm lens at f/5.6, fast shutter freezing the step, natural skin texture, both hands complete with five natural fingers each, natural joint angles, realistic anatomy, no stiff runway walk, no plastic skin.

## Por que funciona

Repetir dois ou três passos e "pousar" sempre no melhor ponto de luz, como sugere a fonte, gera movimento real na roupa e postura sem tensão. O prompt descreve o instante da chegada e o ponto marcado de luz para que a iluminação e a pose fiquem coerentes.

# 438 — Window Light Candid 85mm

## Prompt (copiar)

Candid portrait of a woman in her late 20s with light freckles and natural skin texture, relaxed half-smile, cream knit sweater, sunlit café window seat, soft natural window light, shot on 85mm, shallow depth of field, visible pores, fine film grain.

# 229 — Looking over one shoulder

Finalidade: pose editorial com olhar sobre o ombro para mistério e conexão com o espectador.

## Prompt (copiar)

Create an editorial portrait of an adult person. Their body faces away at a three-quarter angle, while their head turns gently back toward the camera without twisting the shoulders. Keep both arms relaxed at their sides. Frame from the waist upward with space in the direction of the gaze. Use a quiet garden background, diffused daylight, and a calm expression. Keep the neck turn comfortable and the face visible in three-quarter view, photorealistic, 85mm lens.

## Por que funciona

Evita torção forçada do pescoço ao limitar o ângulo do corpo. Espaço na direção do olhar melhora a composição.

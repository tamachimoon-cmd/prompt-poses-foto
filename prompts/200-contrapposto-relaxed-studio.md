# 200 — Contrapposto relaxed studio

Finalidade: pose em pé descontraída com peso deslocado para lookbook ou editorial de moda.

## Prompt (copiar)

Full-length studio photograph of an adult model in her early 30s standing on a light grey seamless backdrop, weight shifted onto the back leg in a relaxed contrapposto, opposite knee softly bent, one thumb lightly hooked at the hip, the other arm relaxed with a small gap from the torso, shoulders dropped, chin level with a soft confident smile toward camera, wearing a tailored cream blazer over a simple top and dark tailored trousers, large softbox key light from camera-left with subtle fill from the right, 70mm lens at f/5.6, camera at waist height, true-to-life skin texture and fabric detail, sharp eyes, both feet fully visible, natural hands, no locked knees, photorealistic editorial photography.

## Por que funciona

O contrapposto cria linha e assimetria natural. O polegar no quadril dá tarefa à mão sem exagero. Iluminação softbox controlada evita sombras duras.

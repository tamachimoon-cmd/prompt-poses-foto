# 397 — Tokyo rain street candid

Finalidade: retrato de rua com chuva, neon e movimento, estilo reportagem.

## Prompt (copiar)

Candid street photo of a woman in her late 20s walking through Tokyo in light rain, neon signs reflecting in puddles, natural posture mid-stride, slight motion blur on feet, shot on Leica Q3 with 35mm lens, documentary style, moody atmosphere, Kodak Portra 400 film grain, realistic wet skin texture.

## Por que funciona

Motion blur nos pés + pele molhada + reflexos de neon quebram a perfeição congelada típica de IA. Portra 400 ancora a cor e o grão.

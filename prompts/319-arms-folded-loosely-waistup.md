# 319 — Arms Folded Loosely Waist-Up

Finalidade: pose profissional acessível sem parecer rígida.

## Prompt (copiar)

Professional waist-up portrait of an adult model in work outfit. Faces camera with slight waist turn. Arms folded loosely at lower chest, one hand resting on the opposite upper arm, other hand visible near the elbow. Shoulders relaxed, chin neutral. Soft studio light, plain background, approachable expression, clear separation between forearms, realistic photography.

Cruzamento solto das braços transmite confiança sem agressividade; mãos visíveis evitam problemas anatômicos.

# 513 — Walking mid-stride street

Finalidade: pose de movimento natural em rua.

Fórmula: mid-stride + braços opostos + lente 35mm + luz diurna + grão.

## Prompt (copiar)

Full-body street-style photo of an adult walking across a quiet pedestrian plaza, captured when the front foot has just touched the ground and the back heel is lifted, arms swinging gently in opposite directions to the legs, head upright and gaze ahead, photographed from a slight side angle with the whole body and both shoes visible, leave space in front of the subject, soft daylight, simple background, 35mm lens, natural skin texture, fine grain.

## Por que funciona

Capturar o meio da passada evita pose estática. Braços em oposição às pernas dão naturalidade.

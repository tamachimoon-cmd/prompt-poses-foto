# 229 — Street portrait overcast

Finalidade: retrato de rua candid com luz difusa para look documental.

## Prompt (copiar)

Street portrait of an adult model on a busy city pavement, background crowd blurred, soft overcast light. 50mm at f/1.8, natural colours, the subject looking straight into the lens with a slight smile, hands in pockets or relaxed, weight shifted, documentary feel, natural skin texture, slight imperfect framing.

## Por que funciona

Luz overcast é suave e lisonjeira. 50mm + f/1.8 isola sem distorção. Pose casual com mãos nos bolsos adiciona naturalidade.

# 236 — Fashion Editorial Over-Shoulder

Finalidade: editorial de moda com olhar sobre o ombro e ângulo dramático.

## Prompt (copiar)

Fashion editorial portrait of an adult model turning body away from camera and looking back over the shoulder with intense gaze, dynamic contrapposto, full body or three-quarter, high fashion wardrobe, dramatic three-quarter angle, Dutch angle composition, Vogue style, soft studio lighting with rim, 85mm lens, photorealistic skin, editorial photography.

## Por que funciona

A pose over-the-shoulder cria tensão e interesse visual imediato; termos de moda e composição guiam o estilo editorial.

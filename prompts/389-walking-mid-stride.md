# 389 — Walking Mid-Stride

Finalidade: pose em movimento de caminhada para capturar energia e naturalidade em street ou runway.

## Prompt (copiar)

Photorealistic full-body photograph of an adult model walking naturally toward the camera mid-stride, one foot forward, arms in natural swinging motion, looking slightly to the side or at the camera, in a city street or open outdoor path, realistic photography style, natural daylight with mild motion suggestion, 50mm lens at f/2.8, eye-level camera, true-to-life proportions and clothing movement, both feet visible in the stride, candid yet controlled lifestyle mood.

## Por que funciona

A meia-passada captura dinamismo sem blur excessivo. Olhar lateral adiciona contexto. Movimento natural de braços evita rigidez de pose estática.

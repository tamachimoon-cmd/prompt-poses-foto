# 423 — Hands in Pockets Casual City

Finalidade: pose relaxada e confiante para retratos casuais urbanos.

## Prompt (copiar)

Photorealistic full-body photograph of an adult model standing with hands in pockets, weight shifted to one leg in slight contrapposto, relaxed confident expression looking at camera, wearing casual jacket and jeans, against cityscape or urban backdrop, natural daylight, 35mm or 50mm lens, eye-level, natural skin and fabric texture, photorealistic lifestyle photography.

## Por que funciona

Mãos nos bolsos resolvem anatomia de mãos e comunicam descontração. Deslocamento de peso evita rigidez.

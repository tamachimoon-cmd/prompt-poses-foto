# 201 — Documentary Harbor Elderly

Finalidade: retrato documental com rosto envelhecido e ambiente real.

## Prompt (copiar)

Documentary portrait of an elderly man at a harbor, weathered face with deep wrinkles and salt-crusted beard, sea in soft focus behind him, overcast daylight, natural skin tones, film photography aesthetic, Kodak Portra 400 look, 85mm lens, candid expression looking off-frame, visible skin texture, no gloss.

## Por que funciona

Luz overcast + filme Portra + detalhes de textura (weathered, salt-crusted) forçam realismo. Evita "hyperrealistic" ou "8K".

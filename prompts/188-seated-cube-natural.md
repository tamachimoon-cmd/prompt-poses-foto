# 188 — Seated Cube Natural

Finalidade: pose sentada natural para editorial ou catálogo.

## Prompt (copiar)

Full-body or 3/4 shot of an adult model sitting on a simple white cube or bench, hands resting gently on knees, legs crossed at ankles, leaning slightly forward, soft natural lighting, minimal background, 85mm lens, sharp eyes, natural posture, editorial photography.

## Por que funciona

Cubo simples isola o sujeito; inclinação e cruzar tornozelos dão naturalidade sem pose forçada.

# 226 — Golden hour rim light outdoor

## Prompt (copiar)

Environmental portrait of an adult model standing in a park at golden hour, low sun behind and slightly to the side creating strong rim light on hair and shoulders, warm golden backlight, soft fill on the face from bounce, 85mm lens at f/1.8, shallow depth of field, blurred trees and grass in background, relaxed natural stance with weight on one leg, looking slightly off-camera, realistic skin texture, Kodak Portra 400 color grade, editorial lifestyle photography.

# 192 — Ombro à frente, cabeça inclinada para o lado oposto

Finalidade: headshot relaxado com sombra nítida na linha da mandíbula.

## Prompt (copiar)

Head-and-shoulders portrait of an adult model in her late 20s, body turned three-quarters, the near (left) shoulder brought slightly forward and down toward the camera, head gently tilted in the opposite direction toward camera-right, chin forward and slightly down, back shoulder level, warm natural smile with relaxed eyes, olive linen blazer over a cream top, warm beige seamless background, medium octabox key placed on camera-right on the same side the head tilts toward, creating a crisp shadow line along the jaw, subtle fill from a silver reflector, 85mm lens at f/4, eyes tack sharp, realistic skin texture, corporate-editorial headshot, no dropped back shoulder, no head thrown back while smiling.

## Por que funciona

Ombro à frente e cabeça para o lado oposto quebram a simetria rígida sem parecer pose de moda. Colocar a luz do lado para onde a cabeça inclina desenha uma sombra limpa na mandíbula. Lembrar "chin forward" no sorriso evita a cabeça indo para trás ao rir.

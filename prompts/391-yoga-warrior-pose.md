# 391 — Yoga Warrior Pose

Finalidade: pose de yoga warrior para equilíbrio, força e atmosfera serena em ambiente natural.

## Prompt (copiar)

Photorealistic full-body photograph of an athletic adult woman in warrior II (virabhadrasana) yoga pose, front knee bent, back leg straight, arms extended parallel to the ground, gaze over the front hand, on a quiet beach or terrace at sunrise, soft pink and orange sky, light mist or haze, glowing rim light, simple neutral activewear, 35mm lens at eye level, sharp focus, natural shadows, realistic color and skin texture, clean composition.

## Por que funciona

A pose warrior tem linhas claras e equilíbrio definido. Ambiente de sunrise com rim light adiciona atmosfera sem competir. Braços estendidos dão escala e força visual.

# 337 — Window light kitchen portrait

Finalidade: retrato natural com luz de janela e textura de pele honesta.

## Prompt (copiar)

Portrait of a woman in her sixties leaning on a kitchen counter with a cup of coffee, soft overcast light from a window on the left, visible pores and fine lines, a few loose gray hairs, everyday clutter softly blurred in the background, 85mm lens at f/2, eye level, natural colors, unretouched skin, photorealistic.

## Por que funciona

Luz de janela lateral + imperfeições explícitas (poros, fios soltos, clutter) matam o look plástico. 85mm isola o sujeito sem achatar demais.

# 175 — Golden hour na rua

Finalidade: externo com rim quente.

## Prompt (copiar)

Outdoor three-quarter portrait of an adult model at golden hour, low sun behind creating a warm rim light in the hair, gentle fill on the face, denim jacket, standing on a city street, weight on the back leg, one hand loose at the side, relaxed smile, field or street softly blurred, 85mm at f/1.8, warm tones, natural skin, candid and unposed, both ears optional, no extra arms.

## Por que funciona

Sol atrás é rim; fill no rosto evita silhueta morta. 85mm f/1.8 separa o fundo.

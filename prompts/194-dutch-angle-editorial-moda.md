# 194 — Dutch angle em editorial de moda

Finalidade: enquadramento inclinado para criar tensão e dinamismo em editorial de moda urbano.

## Prompt (copiar)

Full-length fashion editorial photograph of an adult model in his early 30s on an empty city crosswalk, camera rotated about 15 degrees on its axis so the horizon and the white crosswalk stripes run diagonally through the frame, model standing tall with weight on the left leg, right hand adjusting the collar of a long charcoal overcoat, left hand in the trouser pocket with the thumb outside, chin level, direct gaze into the lens, black turtleneck and leather boots, overcast late afternoon light with soft directional fill from camera-left, 35mm lens at f/4, model kept fully in frame and sharp, deep but natural contrast, editorial street fashion, no fisheye distortion, no motion blur, both hands complete.

## Por que funciona

Dutch angle (canted shot) inclina a linha do horizonte e gera desequilíbrio intencional. Usar linhas fortes (faixas da rua) torna a inclinação legível. Manter postura reta e olhar fixo dá um ponto estável dentro do quadro inclinado.

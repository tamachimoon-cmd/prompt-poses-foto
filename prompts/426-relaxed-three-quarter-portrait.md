# 426 — Relaxed Three-Quarter Portrait

Finalidade: retrato editorial natural com peso deslocado e mãos visíveis, ideal para lifestyle ou moda casual.

## Prompt (copiar)

Create a realistic editorial portrait of an adult model wearing a rust-colored work jacket and dark trousers. They stand with their weight on the back leg and their torso turned slightly away from the camera. Their head turns gently toward the lens, chin level and shoulders relaxed. Both arms hang naturally with loose hands visible beside the hips. Frame from mid-thigh upward at eye level. Plain warm gray background, soft window light from the left, natural expression and skin texture, photorealistic.

## Por que funciona

Descreve distribuição de peso, rotação de torso e cabeça, posição das mãos e enquadramento. Evita o genérico "confident pose".

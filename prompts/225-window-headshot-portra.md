# 225 — Window headshot Portra

Finalidade: headshot natural com luz de janela para look editorial limpo e realista.

## Prompt (copiar)

Tight head-and-shoulders portrait, 85mm lens, f/1.8, adult subject facing camera with relaxed expression and natural skin texture. Soft north-facing window key light at 45 degrees, white reflector fill, gentle shadow under cheekbone, smooth background bokeh. Full-frame photographic realism with subtle grain and accurate catchlights, Portra 400 look, neutral warm Portra-grade finish. No plastic skin, no over-smoothing.

## Por que funciona

85mm + f/1.8 entrega a compressão clássica de retrato. Luz de janela norte + fill especifica o padrão de sombra real. Portra 400 mantém tons de pele naturais sem saturação artificial.

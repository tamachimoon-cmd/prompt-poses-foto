# 229 — Butterfly lighting beauty close-up

Finalidade: close-up de beleza com iluminação butterfly para beauty, skincare ou editorial de moda.

## Prompt (copiar)

Extreme close-up beauty portrait of an adult model, butterfly lighting with key light directly above creating a small shadow under the nose, soft fill from below, clean white or light gray background, 100mm or 85mm macro-ish lens, sharp focus on eyes and skin texture with visible pores and natural imperfections, delicate highlights on cheekbones, neutral expression or soft smile, high-key clean aesthetic, photorealistic beauty photography, no heavy retouching.

## Por que funciona

Butterfly lighting é clássico de beleza e cria sombra controlada sob o nariz. Close-up força detalhe de pele. High-key limpa e eleva o produto (pele).

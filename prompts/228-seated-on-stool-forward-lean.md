# 228 — Seated on stool forward lean

Finalidade: pose sentada no banco com inclinação para frente, boa para estúdio e contato visual.

## Prompt (copiar)

Create a full-body studio portrait of an adult person wearing a rust-colored work jacket and dark trousers seated near the front of a simple wooden stool. Both feet rest flat on the floor, one slightly ahead of the other. The torso leans forward a little from the hips. Rest each hand loosely on its corresponding thigh, with fingers relaxed and visible. Keep the head upright and gaze toward the camera. Include the entire stool and both shoes, with space below the feet. Soft side lighting and a neutral backdrop, photorealistic.

## Por que funciona

Inclinação suave do tronco e mãos nas coxas dão naturalidade e evitam mãos flutuando. Especificar pés e espaço abaixo previne crops ruins.

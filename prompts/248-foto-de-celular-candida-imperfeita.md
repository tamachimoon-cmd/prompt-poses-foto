# 248 — Foto de celular cândida e imperfeita

Finalidade: lifestyle com cara de foto real de celular, para posts orgânicos e UGC.

## Prompt (copiar)

Candid phone snapshot of two adult friends in their early 30s laughing at a small outdoor café table, waist-up, one mid-sentence gesturing with a coffee cup, the other looking at her and smiling, slightly off-centre crop with a bit of empty table on the right, late-afternoon sun from behind camera-right with a single clear light direction and consistent shadows, 26mm smartphone wide-lens feel at eye level, mild phone HDR, everyday objects in the background (a bike, a menu board out of focus), flyaway hairs, a crease in a linen shirt, natural skin texture with visible pores and uneven tone, realistic eye reflections, true-to-life color, light compression noise, no plastic skin, no beauty filter, no extra fingers, no readable text.

## Por que funciona

Pedir "phone snapshot", enquadramento levemente descentralizado e objetos comuns no fundo afasta a cena encenada demais. As fontes recomendam uma ou duas imperfeições físicas (cabelo solto, vinco) e uma única direção de luz. Remover "perfect/flawless/8k" evita o brilho artificial.
